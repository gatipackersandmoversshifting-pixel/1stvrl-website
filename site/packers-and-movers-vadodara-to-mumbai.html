The truck leaves Vadodara on NH48 near Kapurai or Tarsali and heads south past Karjan to Bharuch, where it crosses the wide Narmada. From there the highway runs through the Ankleshwar industrial belt, bypasses Surat on the Kamrej side and continues through Navsari, Valsad and Vapi to the Maharashtra border at Achhad. Inside Maharashtra it passes Talasari and Manor in Palghar district and enters Mumbai's northern edge near Ghodbunder and Dahisar. The road distance is about 415 km, so this page uses the up to 500 km band of our chart, and household goods usually reach in 1 to 2 days.

There is usually no night halt on this route. A truck loaded in the morning in Vadodara passes Bharuch around midday, clears Surat and Valsad in the afternoon and reaches the Mumbai edge by night, waiting there until the society's unloading hours. NH48 between Ankleshwar and Vapi carries heavy tanker and container traffic, so the driver keeps a steady pace rather than a fast one. In July and August heavy rain on the south Gujarat coast and in Palghar can slow the last stretch. Tolls on NH48 are shown as one line on the estimate.

Moving into Mumbai: what to plan for

A Vadodara bungalow or a roomy Bhayli flat rarely fits into a Mumbai home of the same budget, so the first planning job is to decide what goes. Mumbai societies normally want a letter from the secretary before goods come in, many fix the hours for shifting, and older buildings in the island city and the inner suburbs can have small lifts or none. The Mumbai monsoon from June to September is heavier and more frequent than Vadodara's, so the unloading crew covers the building entrance and the corridor before the first carton is carried in.

Common delivery areas are Borivali, Kandivali, Malad, Goregaon, Andheri, Powai, Bandra, Dadar, Chembur, Ghatkopar, Mulund, Thane West, Mira Road, Vashi and Kharghar.

Our tip for this route: Get the inside size of the Mumbai lift and the width of the flat's main door before the survey. If a sofa or wardrobe will not pass, it is cheaper to sell or give it away in Vadodara than to pay freight on it and find it stuck in the lobby.

Vadodara to Mumbai packers and movers: who takes this road

Executives joining offices in BKC, Lower Parel, Andheri or Powai, staff of banks and insurers moving to a head office, and engineers from the Vadodara refinery and chemical units posted to Mumbai's corporate teams make up a large share of this route. Families are the other half: parents from Alkapuri, Akota or Manjalpur moving to live near a son or daughter in Thane, Borivali or Kandivali, or a young couple taking up a lease in Navi Mumbai.

Because the distance is short and the road is a single highway, people often expect a Vadodara to Mumbai move to be simple. The hard part is usually at the Mumbai end: tight parking, a booked lift, and a building that allows unloading only in the late morning. Packers and movers who plan the Mumbai end first, rather than the loading in Vadodara, tend to deliver with fewer surprises.

Packing a Baroda home for a humid city

Furniture that has stood in Vadodara's dry winters and hot summers meets coastal humidity in Mumbai. Steel cupboards, fridges and washing machines are wrapped in film with padded corners, since scratches rust quickly near the sea, and wooden beds, swings and dining sets get corrugated sheet on every edge. Mattresses travel in thick plastic, and books and clothes go into lined cartons that survive a short spell of rain during unloading.

The last carry in Mumbai is often long, from a compound gate, into a lift and along a corridor. Every carton is numbered and marked with the room it belongs to in the new flat, and fragile cartons with crockery, glass and frames are named on the inventory so you can open and check them first.

Common questions

How much do packers and movers charge from Vadodara to Mumbai?

For about 415 km, a 1 BHK costs Rs 9,500 - 17,000, a 2 BHK Rs 13,000 - 24,000 and a 3 BHK Rs 17,000 - 32,000, plus 18 per cent GST. The written estimate after the survey is the final price.

Can goods from Vadodara reach Mumbai the next day?

Often, yes. A truck loaded in the morning usually reaches the Mumbai edge by night, and unloading is planned for the next morning inside the society's allowed hours. On a shared truck it can take up to 2 days. The coordinator confirms the delivery day when you book.

Do you deliver to Thane, Mira Road, Vasai and Navi Mumbai?

Yes. Mira Road, Vasai and Virar come before Mumbai on this route, and Thane, Vashi, Kharghar and Panvel are on the same up to 500 km chart. Share the society's move-in rules with the coordinator in advance.

What does car shifting from Vadodara to Mumbai cost?

On a car carrier, a hatchback such as a Swift or i20 is about Rs 5,500 to 9,000, a sedan such as a Dzire or Honda City about Rs 6,500 to 10,500 and an SUV or MUV such as an Innova or XUV700 about Rs 9,000 to 14,000, plus GST.

Can I re-register my GJ 06 car in Maharashtra after moving?

If the car will stay in Maharashtra for more than a year, it should be re-registered there. Take an NOC from the Vadodara RTO before you leave and keep the registration certificate, insurance and pollution papers together with the car.

Which papers do Mumbai societies usually ask for?

Mostly a no-objection letter from the society office, a copy of the lease or sale agreement and, for tenants, police verification. Start on these a week before the move so the unloading slot is not held up.

Can part of my Vadodara home wait in storage while I find a bigger flat?

Yes. Pieces that do not fit yet can stay in a partner warehouse near Mumbai, charged separately and shown on the estimate, and be delivered when you are ready.

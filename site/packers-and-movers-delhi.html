Shifting in Delhi: what we plan for

Delhi moves change a lot from one colony to the next. DDA flats and older builder floors often have no lift and narrow stairs, so beds, sofas and fridges are carried by hand and the crew size is set for it. Goods vehicles face daytime entry limits on many Delhi roads, so loading is usually planned for early morning or late evening, and in tight lanes a smaller vehicle shuttles goods to the main truck.

For gated societies in Dwarka, Vasant Kunj and Rohini we book the lift and the gate pass with the RWA a day before. For kothis in South Delhi we send a larger crew because the furniture count is higher. Parking for the truck is checked during the survey so the vehicle is not stuck on a main road while loading.

Common questions

How much do packers and movers charge in Delhi?

A local Delhi move costs Rs 5,000 to 9,000 for a 1 BHK, Rs 8,000 to 15,000 for a 2 BHK and Rs 12,000 to 21,000 for a 3 BHK, plus GST. For a floor without a lift, a stair charge is shown as a separate line on the estimate.

Do you have an office in Delhi?

No. We do not have our own office in Delhi. Delhi moves are handled by a verified partner team and coordinated from our head office at #705, Sector 7, Panchkula. You still get one coordinator, one written estimate and one GST bill from 1st VRL Packers and Movers.

Can you shift a DDA flat with no lift?

Yes. The survey notes the floor and the stair width. Heavy items are carried by hand and the stair charge is shown as its own line on the estimate, so there is no surprise on the day.

When can the truck load in Delhi?

Goods vehicles face daytime entry limits on many Delhi roads, so most loading is done early in the morning or in the evening. We fix the time during the survey based on your colony and your building's rules.

Do you shift from Delhi to other cities?

Yes. The most common routes are Delhi to Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, Kolkata and Chennai. Each route page has its own rate chart for house, car, bike and office moves.

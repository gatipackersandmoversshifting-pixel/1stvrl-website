About us

A shifting company that writes the price down first

1st VRL Packers and Movers runs home and office moves from its offices in Panchkula, Jammu and Shimla, with a fixed written estimate and one coordinator on your number.

How we work

Every move starts with the pickup and drop address, the floor and the volume of goods. We turn that into a written estimate that lists packing, transport, insurance and any extra carry or floor work. Nothing is loaded until you have seen the number.

From VRL Packers and Movers to 1st VRL

We started in Panchkula as VRL Packers and Movers and have been GST registered since April 2023 (GSTIN 06AEYPV2101B1ZM). In October 2026 we changed our name to 1st VRL Packers and Movers, because many unrelated companies across India use VRL in their name. The team, the office and the number are the same. Together with our partner network, we have completed more than 2,000 moves, and the company is ISO 9001:2015 certified. Read about the name change.

The crew arrives with packing materials, moving blankets, stretch film and tools. Furniture is dismantled where it needs to be and re-assembled at the drop. Fragile and high-value items are packed separately and listed on the inventory.

  • Offices in Panchkula, Jammu and Shimla.
  • Trained in-house crew, not day labour picked on the day.
  • Vehicle sealed at loading with the driver contact shared.
  • Written estimate with GST and insurance stated clearly.
  • Registered under GSTIN 06AEYPV2101B1ZM. Every estimate and invoice carries the number.

Bengaluru to Delhi at a glance

2,150 kmRoad distance, approx.
7 to 9 daysHousehold transit time
NH44Main route
1,500 km plusPrice band used below

Leaving Bengaluru, the truck takes Bellary Road out past Hebbal and the airport and stays on NH44 almost all the way north: through Chikkaballapur and Anantapur, across the Tungabhadra at Kurnool, around Hyderabad on the ring road, then through Adilabad into Maharashtra for Nagpur. From there the highway climbs through Seoni and Sagar in Madhya Pradesh, reaches Jhansi and Gwalior, and drops into the plains at Agra before the final stretch to Delhi. The distance is about 2,150 km, which puts this move in the 1,500 km plus price band, the top band on our rate card. On a shared truck household goods normally reach Delhi in 7 to 9 days from loading, while a dedicated vehicle cuts that by a few days at a higher price.

Expect about four days of actual driving. Typical overnight stops are around Kurnool or the Hyderabad bypass, then Nagpur, and then Sagar or Jhansi before the last day through Gwalior and Agra. The Deccan stretch through Anantapur and Kurnool is hot and dry for most of the year, and in May and June the Vidarbha belt around Nagpur is one of the hottest parts of the trip, so drivers prefer early starts. There are many toll plazas on NH44, and the full toll amount is shown as one line on the estimate rather than charged on the way. Because goods spend more than a week in transit, cartons get double corners, mattresses are bagged and the load is sealed in front of you in Bengaluru.

Moving into Delhi: what to plan for

Delhi homes are usually larger than Bengaluru apartments but harder to reach. Builder floors in areas like Malviya Nagar, Rajouri Garden and Lajpat Nagar are often three or four storeys with a narrow staircase and no lift, so a big wardrobe or a double-door fridge goes up by hand and the crew is sized for that. Many roads are closed to trucks during the day, so the vehicle from Bengaluru normally waits at the city border and unloads early in the morning or after dark. Weather is the other shock after Bengaluru's mild climate. From April to June the heat is fierce and dusty, and from December to February the cold and fog arrive, so electronics are packed tight against dust and the delivery day is kept flexible in winter. Moves to Gurugram, Noida, Faridabad and Ghaziabad usually end in high-rise societies with service lifts, which makes unloading quicker.

Common delivery areas are Dwarka, Janakpuri, Rajouri Garden, Rohini, Pitampura, Saket, Malviya Nagar, Vasant Kunj, Mayur Vihar, Gurugram (Golf Course Road, Sohna Road), Noida, Greater Noida West, Faridabad and Ghaziabad.

Our tip for this route: Before the truck leaves Bengaluru, confirm the exact parking spot at your Delhi address, whether it is the society basement, the colony gate or the main road. If the truck has to stop far from the door, we arrange extra hands or a smaller vehicle in advance instead of losing a delivery morning.

Common questions

How much do packers and movers charge from Bengaluru to Delhi?

For about 2,150 km, a 1 BHK costs Rs 20,000 - 35,000, a 2 BHK Rs 27,000 - 47,000 and a 3 BHK Rs 34,000 - 60,000, plus 18 per cent GST. The written estimate after the survey is the final price.

How many days does Bengaluru to Delhi shifting take?

Plan for 7 to 9 days on a shared truck from the day of loading. In December and January, fog north of Jhansi and around Agra can add a day, and the coordinator tells you before the truck is late, not after.

What does it cost to send a car from Bengaluru to Delhi?

On a car carrier a hatchback such as a Swift costs Rs 13,000 to 22,000, a sedan Rs 15,000 to 25,000, an SUV or MUV like an Innova or XUV700 Rs 20,000 to 34,000, and a luxury car on an enclosed carrier Rs 40,000 to 65,000, plus GST.

Can you move a small office from Bengaluru to Delhi NCR?

Yes. An office of up to 10 workstations costs Rs 55,000 to 90,000 and a medium office of 10 to 30 workstations Rs 1,10,000 to 2,00,000 on this route, plus GST. Desk dismantling and reinstallation is billed per workstation.

Can I send my Royal Enfield from Bengaluru to Delhi?

Yes. A Classic 350 or Bullet costs Rs 7,000 to 10,000, and a 650cc twin or Himalayan Rs 10,000 to 14,500 on this route, plus GST. The bike is padded, strapped upright and listed on the inventory.

Do you handle defence and central government transfers to Delhi?

Yes. You get an itemised GST invoice, an inventory list and the lorry receipt, which most transfer claims ask for. For bank and PSU transfers the move is arranged through our IBA-approved partner (code MUK-2068).

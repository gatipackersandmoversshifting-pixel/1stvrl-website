Moving in and around Shimla

Shimla moves need a different plan from a move on the plains. Hill roads, narrow lanes and long carries from the road to the door decide the vehicle size and the crew count. Our Shimla team surveys the access first, plans the carry from the nearest point a truck can stand, and packs every item for the climb and the descent on the highway.

Areas we serve: Sanjauli, Dhalli, Chhota Shimla, Kasumpti, New Shimla, Khalini, Boileauganj, Totu, Summer Hill, Lakkar Bazar, Mashobra, Kufri and Shoghi, along with intercity moves from Shimla to Chandigarh, Delhi and the rest of India.

How the job runs

Every move runs on the same six steps. Nothing is left to the day.

  1. Survey. A surveyor counts the inventory room by room and notes the lift, the floor and where the truck can stand. Local surveys can run over a video call.
  2. Written estimate. The quote separates packing material, labour, transport, tolls and insurance into their own lines. The total is the number held at delivery.
  3. Packing day. Cartons are labelled by room. Beds and wardrobes come apart. Glass and screens get two layers of wrap and are photographed before the wrap goes on.
  4. Loading. The crew loads inside the building's working window and the vehicle is sealed in front of you. The driver contact is shared before it leaves.
  5. Transit. The coordinator updates you at each halt and the driver calls an hour out from the destination.
  6. Delivery. Each item goes to the room the label names. Furniture is reassembled where it stands. The receipt is signed only after the inventory has been checked.

Common questions

How much do packers and movers cost?

A local move costs Rs 3,000 for a single room, Rs 5,000 to 9,000 for a 1 BHK, Rs 8,000 to 15,000 for a 2 BHK and Rs 12,000 to 21,000 for a 3 BHK. Intercity moves start near Rs 9,000 for 300 km. All figures exclude 18 per cent GST.

What is included in the quoted price?

Complete packing, labour for loading and unloading, transport in a sized vehicle, one flight of stairs served by a working lift, and unpacking at the destination. Tolls, permits, insurance and GST are separate lines on the same sheet.

How quickly will you call back?

Within 30 minutes between 8 AM and 10 PM. Submit the form above or call +91 82640 91243 and a coordinator will price your move over the phone or book a free survey.

How much advance should I pay?

Ten to twenty per cent of the estimate is the normal advance in India. The balance is paid at delivery after the inventory has been checked against the list that left the pickup. A demand for most of the payment up front is a risk.

Is transit insurance included?

Basic handling protection is included. Comprehensive transit cover costs 2 to 3 per cent of the declared value and is quoted as its own line so you can see exactly what it costs.

How do I verify a moving company?

Check the GSTIN on the GST portal and confirm the invoice comes from the same company. Ask whether the crew and the vehicle are in-house. Ask for the insurance terms in writing. Take a written, itemised estimate before any advance is paid.

Do you provide packing materials?

Yes. Packing material is charged at cost on its own line. A 2 BHK uses 25 to 35 cartons, stretch wrap, bubble wrap for fragile items, furniture blankets and mattress covers.

What is not allowed on the truck?

LPG cylinders, fuel, batteries, paint and solvents cannot travel. Cash, jewellery, passports and documents stay with you. Plants and loose luggage can travel if they are packed and listed on the inventory.

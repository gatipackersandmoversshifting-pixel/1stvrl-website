Shifting in Patiala: what we plan for

Patiala is a city of large old homes and planned estates. Model Town, Urban Estate and the Bhupindra Road side have independent houses with gardens and plenty of furniture, while the walled old city has narrow bazaars where a truck cannot enter. University and college staff, doctors and government employees make up a good share of moves, often between Patiala and Chandigarh or Delhi. Summers are hot and the monsoon is heavy, so we pack wood and fabric against heat and wet.

Big houses in Model Town and Urban Estate

A Patiala kothi often has more furniture than its size suggests: heavy beds, showcases, dining sets and a store room full of trunks. The survey lists every room, including the store and the terrace, so the truck and crew are sized right the first time.

Old furniture with carved wood or glass doors is wrapped in blankets and corner guards, and glass panels are packed separately. If some goods are staying behind with family, we label them clearly at the survey so nothing goes on the truck by mistake.

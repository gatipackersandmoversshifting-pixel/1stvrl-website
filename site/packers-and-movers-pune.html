Shifting in Pune: what we plan for

Pune is really two cities for a mover. The old core, with the peths around Shaniwar Wada such as Sadashiv Peth, Narayan Peth and Kasba Peth, has wadas and small buildings on narrow lanes where a big truck cannot turn, so goods are carried to a smaller vehicle or the truck stands on the nearest wide road. The newer side, spread from Hinjewadi, Wakad and Baner in the west to Kharadi, Wagholi and Hadapsar in the east, is mostly housing societies with lifts, security gates and committee rules about move timings. Distances across the Pune region are long, and roads around Hinjewadi, Kharadi and the University junction slow to a crawl at office hours, so every move is planned around traffic as much as around packing.

During the survey we note the floor, the lift size, the society's allowed hours and where the truck can stand. West-side moves between Baner, Balewadi, Aundh and Pashan are usually loaded before 9 am or after the evening rush. In the old city the crew is larger, because more items go down the stairs by hand. From June to September Pune gets steady monsoon rain, heavier on the hills towards Bavdhan and Sinhagad Road, so every carton is stretch-wrapped and the truck is covered before it leaves the gate. Summer moves from March to May are hot and dry, and electronics are padded against heat and dust instead.

Common questions

How much do packers and movers charge in Pune?

Within Pune city a 1 RK costs Rs 3,000 to 6,500, a 1 BHK Rs 5,000 to 9,000, a 2 BHK Rs 8,000 to 15,000, a 3 BHK Rs 12,000 to 21,000 and a 4 BHK or bungalow Rs 17,000 to 29,000, plus GST. A move between Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad is priced on the same local band, for example Rs 8,000 to 15,000 plus GST for a 2 BHK. Stairs in a building with no lift are shown as a separate charge on the estimate.

Do you have an office in Pune?

No. We do not have an office, branch or warehouse in Pune. Pune moves are carried out by a verified partner team and coordinated from our head office at #705, Sector 7, Panchkula. You still deal with one coordinator and get one written estimate and one GST bill from 1st VRL Packers and Movers.

Can you shift from an old peth building in Pune?

Yes. Tell us the lane width, the floor and the staircase during the survey. If the truck cannot reach the door, a smaller vehicle shuttles goods to it, and any extra stair or shuttle charge is written on the estimate.

Does Pune's monsoon affect shifting?

Moves run all year. From June to September cartons and furniture get waterproof wrap, the truck carries a tarpaulin, and loading is planned between heavy spells where possible.

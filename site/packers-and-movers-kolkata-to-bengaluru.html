Kolkata to Bengaluru at a glance

1,880 kmRoad distance, approx.
6 to 8 daysHousehold transit time
NH16Main route
1,500 km plusPrice band used below

The truck leaves Kolkata across the Hooghly and joins NH16 for the run to Kharagpur, then follows the east coast through Balasore, Cuttack and Bhubaneswar in Odisha and Visakhapatnam, Rajahmundry and Vijayawada in Andhra Pradesh. In the south of Andhra Pradesh it leaves the coast and climbs inland to the Bengaluru plateau. The road distance is about 1,880 km, which puts this move in the 1,500 km plus price band, and household goods usually reach in 6 to 8 days on a shared truck.

Most trucks take four days of driving, halting usually near Bhubaneswar or Cuttack, near Srikakulam, and near Vijayawada or Ongole before the final day inland. NH16 is a four- and six-lane freight corridor with frequent toll plazas, and the total toll is shown once on the estimate. Big river bridges over the Mahanadi, Godavari and Krishna come one after another. The coastal belt sees heavy rain from June to September and a cyclone risk later in the year, so a spare tarpaulin rides in the cabin, and the air turns cooler and drier only on the last climb to Bengaluru.

Moving into Bengaluru: what to plan for

Most Kolkata to Bengaluru moves end in apartment complexes run by an owners' association, and almost every one has a move-in procedure: a written or online request, a fixed slot, a lift booking and sometimes a deposit. Bengaluru also limits heavy vehicles on many city roads during the day, so the truck often waits at the city edge and the partner team in Bengaluru unloads in the slot your association gives. The weather is milder and far less humid than Kolkata, but September and October can bring heavy evening showers.

Common delivery areas are Whitefield, Marathahalli, Bellandur, Sarjapur Road, HSR Layout, Koramangala, BTM Layout, Electronic City, JP Nagar, Hebbal, Thanisandra, Yelahanka, KR Puram and Hennur.

Our tip for this route: Ask your Bengaluru association for the move-in form as soon as you sign the lease. Some want it several days ahead, and a truck that arrives without an approved slot may have to wait outside the gate.

Why Kolkata families move to Bengaluru, and how we pack for it

Most people booking Kolkata to Bengaluru packers and movers are joining an IT company or a startup, often on a relocation allowance with a fixed budget. Others are parents moving to live near children who already work in Bengaluru, bringing a household built over decades. The two moves look very different. A young professional may have a bed, a fridge, a washing machine and twenty cartons, while a parents' home has heavy wooden furniture, a showcase full of glass and several almirahs, and the survey sizes the truck share for each.

The coastal road is humid for most of the trip and the Bay of Bengal can turn rough in the cyclone months, so packing is done for damp as much as for distance. Electronics go in their own padded cartons, the fridge travels upright and dry, wooden furniture is wrapped in foam and stretch film, and mattresses travel in sealed plastic bags. Glass shelves are taken out of showcases, wrapped in bubble sheet and packed flat in a separate marked carton. The load is then covered with a tarpaulin under the truck's own cover.

Common questions

How much do packers and movers charge from Kolkata to Bengaluru?

For about 1,880 km, a 1 BHK costs Rs 20,000 - 35,000, a 2 BHK Rs 27,000 - 47,000 and a 3 BHK Rs 34,000 - 60,000, plus 18 per cent GST. The written estimate after the survey is the final price.

How long does Kolkata to Bengaluru shifting take?

Plan on 6 to 8 days from the day of loading. If a cyclone warning is issued on the coast while the truck is on the road, the driver waits at a safe halt, and the coordinator informs you and gives a revised delivery date.

Can you deliver to Whitefield and Electronic City apartments?

Yes. These areas have large complexes with service lifts and security teams that want the crew list and vehicle number in advance. The coordinator sends these to your association, books the slot and confirms the time with you the day before delivery.

Can a student shift a single room from Kolkata to Bengaluru?

Yes. A room with a bed, a study table, a few cartons and a cycle is priced as a 1 RK, Rs 18,000 to 32,000 plus GST on this route. A shared truck keeps the cost near the lower figure, and a scooter can travel with it for Rs 4,500 to 7,000 plus GST.

Can you pack a large TV and a refrigerator for the Bengaluru trip?

Yes. The TV goes in its original box or a padded TV carton with corner guards, and the fridge is cleaned, dried, taped shut and carried upright. Both are listed on the inventory with their condition noted at loading in Kolkata, and checked again at your Bengaluru door.

What if I am already in Bengaluru when the goods are packed in Kolkata?

That is common. A family member or friend can be present at packing to check the inventory, or you can join on a video call while the partner team packs. The signed inventory is shared with you, and you check it against the goods when they reach Bengaluru.

Can you move a medium office from Kolkata to Bengaluru?

Yes. An office of 10 to 30 workstations costs Rs 1,10,000 to 2,00,000 plus GST on this route, with desks dismantled and refitted at Rs 800 to 2,000 each. Computers are packed by desk number, and the GST invoice can be raised in the company's name.

Shifting in Goniana: what we plan for

Goniana, also called Goniana Mandi, is a grain market town in the Malwa plains a short drive from Bathinda city. National Highway 54 passes through it, so most trucks leave the town straight onto the highway towards Bathinda in one direction and Kotkapura and Faridkot in the other, and the town also has a station on the Bathinda to Ferozepur railway line. Its anaj mandi handles wheat, paddy and cotton from nearby villages, and commission agents, traders and farmers make up much of the population. Many people commute to jobs in Bathinda, which keeps rented houses in steady demand. Goniana is known for its places of worship, including Tikana Bhai Jagta Ji Gurdwara, the Durga Mata temple and the Ichhapuran Hanuman temple. Homes include older houses in the bazaar, new kothis in colonies off the highway and plots on the edge of town. Summers are scorching, and winter fog on NH54 can be heavy.

Fog on NH54 and timing the delivery

From mid-December to January, fog along NH54 can stay thick until late morning. Long-distance trucks start once visibility improves, and the delivery window you get allows for slower driving.

In harvest weeks, tractor trolleys crowd the roads near the mandi. If your house is close to the market, the coordinator suggests an early start so loading finishes before the trolleys arrive. Summer moves follow the same idea: the hottest hours of May and June are kept for driving, not for carrying furniture down stairs.

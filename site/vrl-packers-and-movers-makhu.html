Shifting in Makhu: what we plan for

Makhu is a nagar panchayat town in Ferozepur district, close to the point where the Beas joins the Sutlej, about 5 km away, and on the edge of the Harike wetland, one of the largest in northern India. Three national highways meet near the town, including NH54 towards Harike and Amritsar and NH703B towards Moga, and Makhu railway station is on the line between Ferozepur and Jalandhar, with trains towards Lohian Khas and Jalandhar on one side and Mallanwala and Ferozepur on the other. Zira lies a short drive to the south. The town has a busy main road lined with shops, workshops, hospitals and dhabas serving highway traffic, a grain market, and villages such as Sadarwala, Mohkamwala and Variah within easy reach. Much of the land near the rivers is low-lying, so the monsoon matters here more than in most of Punjab. Homes are a mix of older houses off the bazaar and newer kothis on the highway approaches.

Makhu and Zira on the local band

Zira is roughly 15 km from Makhu by road, inside the same Ferozepur district, so moves between the two are local. The same applies to moves within Makhu and to its nearby villages. A 2 BHK is roughly Rs 8,000 to 15,000 plus GST and a 3 BHK roughly Rs 12,000 to 21,000 plus GST.

Ferozepur city is in the same district but well over 30 km from Makhu by road, so it is priced on the up to 500 km band, as are Moga, Tarn Taran, Jalandhar and Amritsar, which are in other districts. On that band a 2 BHK is roughly Rs 13,000 to 24,000 plus GST.

Packers and movers Makhu near the rivers

Villages between Makhu, the Harike headworks and the river banks sit on low ground. After heavy rain some link roads stay soft for days, so the coordinator checks the access road with you before the date is fixed, and the partner team keeps a smaller vehicle in mind if the main truck cannot reach the house safely.

Inside the truck, wooden furniture gets a plastic sheet under the blanket wrap, mattresses go into corrugated sheet and film, and cartons are taped on every seam. Electronics are packed in their own boxes or in padded cartons and kept off the floor of the truck.

Highway junction loading

Makhu is a junction where long-distance trucks, buses and farm traffic meet, and the main road through town is busy most of the day. Loading is planned for the early morning, the truck is parked off the carriageway, and a team member watches traffic while large pieces cross to the vehicle.

For homes in the older lanes off the bazaar, the truck waits on the main road and goods are carried out by hand or on trolleys. The carry distance is noted at the survey and built into the estimate.

Home shifting in Makhu from monsoon to fog

July and August are the months to plan around. Home shifting in Makhu during the monsoon goes ahead on dry mornings where possible, with sheets laid at both doors so mud is not tracked onto floors or furniture. If a day turns too wet to load safely, the date is moved by agreement rather than risking the goods.

Winters bring fog over the river flats and the highway. Short moves to Zira usually go ahead with a later start, while trucks heading to Delhi or further leave once visibility is safe, and the delivery window you receive is written with that in mind.

Mumbai to Hyderabad at a glance

710 kmRoad distance, approx.
3 to 5 daysHousehold transit time
NH65 via SolapurMain route
500 to 1,500 kmPrice band used below

From Mumbai the truck takes the expressway to Pune, then runs east on NH65 through Indapur and Solapur and enters Telangana near Zaheerabad before reaching Hyderabad. At about 710 km it sits in the 500 to 1,500 km price band. On a shared truck household goods normally reach in 3 to 5 days, and a dedicated vehicle can do it in about two days.

The trip usually takes two driving days with one overnight halt near Solapur. The Pune to Solapur section of NH65 is a wide four-lane road, so the run is steady, and the truck crosses into Telangana on the second day. Toll on the expressway and NH65 is shown as a single line on the estimate, and the driver calls you about an hour before he reaches the gate in Hyderabad.

Moving into Hyderabad: what to plan for

Newer Hyderabad neighbourhoods are built around gated communities, especially in Gachibowli, Kondapur, Nanakramguda, Kokapet and Kukatpally. Associations generally want a move-in request in advance, allot a lift and a time window, and check the vehicle at the gate. The city is dry for much of the year, but heavy showers in July and August can flood underpasses, so the route inside the city is chosen on the day.

Common delivery areas are Gachibowli, Kondapur, HITEC City, Madhapur, Nanakramguda, Kokapet, Manikonda, Kukatpally, Miyapur, Begumpet and Secunderabad.

Our tip for this route: Send us your Hyderabad community's move-in rules as soon as you have them. Most ask for the vehicle number and crew details a day ahead, and we share those once the truck is assigned.

Does my employer pay for Mumbai to Hyderabad shifting?

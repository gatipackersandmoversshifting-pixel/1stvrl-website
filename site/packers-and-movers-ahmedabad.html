Shifting in Ahmedabad: what we plan for

Home shifting in Ahmedabad changes a lot from one bank of the Sabarmati to the other. West of the river, Satellite, Bodakdev, Prahlad Nagar, Thaltej and the towers along SG Highway and Sindhu Bhavan Road are mostly gated societies with lifts, a security cabin and a chairman or office that wants a move logged in advance. East of the river, in Maninagar, Naroda, Nikol, Vastral and Bapunagar, many families live in tenements, the local name for row houses, or in older walk-up blocks with a steep staircase and no lift. The walled old city, with its pols and khadkis, has lanes too narrow for any truck. Heat is the other big factor: from April to June afternoons often cross 43 degrees, which is hard on the crew and on polished wood and electronics, so most Ahmedabad moves are loaded in the early morning.

Every booking starts with a survey, by video call or at your door, where the partner team notes the floor, the lift size, the width of the lane and where the truck can stand. In the high-rise belt of Bodakdev, Vastrapur and South Bopal, the team brings lift padding and floor sheets and works inside the slot the society gives. For tenements in Maninagar or Nikol, the bigger job is usually a narrow internal staircase, so beds and wardrobes are taken apart upstairs. In the pols of Khadia, Raipur and Dariapur, goods are carried by hand to a small tempo, which shuttles them to a truck waiting on a main road such as Relief Road. Large goods vehicles face time limits on many city roads, so the coordinator fixes the loading hour with that in mind.

Home shifting in Ahmedabad: what each area needs

Packers and movers in Satellite, Prahlad Nagar and Vastrapur mostly deal with apartment towers of eight to fourteen floors, built over the last twenty years, where the lift slot and the society's move hours decide the day. Bodakdev, Thaltej and Sindhu Bhavan Road have many larger 3 and 4 BHK flats and some bungalows, so the furniture count is higher and a second crew is sometimes needed. Further out, South Bopal, Shela and the Ghuma side are full of newer societies with wide internal roads, which makes truck parking easy, but the long walk from tower to gate means trolleys are sent for the cartons.

In the north of the city, Chandkheda, Motera, Gota and Ghatlodia have a mix of societies and tenements, and many residents work in Gandhinagar, so moves there are often timed around a government or GIFT City joining date. Paldi, Navrangpura and Ellisbridge are older and central, with low-rise blocks, bungalows and limited daytime parking. On the eastern side, Maninagar, Naroda, Nikol and Vastral are busy residential belts close to the GIDC industrial estates, where the roads are good but tenement staircases are narrow. The survey records all of this, so the crew size and vehicle are right on the day.

Office and shop shifting in Ahmedabad

Most Ahmedabad offices sit along SG Highway, the Corporate Road in Prahlad Nagar, Sindhu Bhavan Road, Ashram Road and C G Road, with factories and warehouses in the Naroda, Vatva and Odhav industrial estates and around Changodar and Sanand. Commercial buildings usually let goods in only through a service entrance outside working hours, and the facility team often wants the crew's names and a written item list a day before. Office moves are therefore planned for a Saturday evening or a Sunday, so the team can work on Monday morning. Shops on C G Road or in the old markets around Relief Road need a parking plan for the truck, which the coordinator agrees in advance.

An office move within Ahmedabad is charged on the local office card: up to 10 workstations costs about Rs 12,000 to 25,000, 10 to 30 workstations about Rs 25,000 to 60,000 and 30 to 100 workstations about Rs 60,000 to 1,50,000, all plus GST. Anything bigger is priced after a site visit. Dismantling and refitting a workstation is billed at Rs 800 to 2,000 per desk and a server rack at Rs 3,000 to 8,000. Computers are packed in cartons labelled by desk number, and cables are bagged with each machine so every team can start work quickly.

Uttarayan, Navratri, heat and the right time to move

Ahmedabad's calendar shapes moving dates more than most cities. Around Uttarayan on 14 and 15 January the whole city is on its rooftops flying kites, the old city lanes are packed and loose kite string across roads is a real hazard for the crew, so it is best to move a few days before or after. Navratri in September or October brings late-night garba events and heavy traffic near the big venues, and the Diwali weeks are the busiest time of year for trucks and labour. Booking ten days ahead in these seasons usually gets you the date you want.

Summer is the hardest season. From April to June the partner team starts loading soon after sunrise, keeps wooden furniture out of direct sun and wraps electronics twice, and it helps if drinking water and a fan are ready at both homes. The monsoon, usually from late June or July to September, is shorter and lighter than Mumbai's, but a heavy spell can flood underpasses and low roads within an hour, so mattresses and sofas go into plastic and the truck waits on a dry stretch if needed. October to March, outside the festival weeks, is the most comfortable time to shift.

Common questions

What does a house move within Ahmedabad cost?

Inside Ahmedabad city, for example Nikol to Bodakdev or Paldi to Chandkheda, a 1 BHK costs about Rs 5,000 to 9,000, a 2 BHK about Rs 8,000 to 15,000 and a 3 BHK about Rs 12,000 to 21,000, plus GST. The written estimate after the survey gives one final figure, and stairs or a long carry are shown on their own line.

Do you have an office in Ahmedabad?

No. We have no office or branch in Ahmedabad. Moves here are done by a verified partner team and coordinated from our head office at #705, Sector-7, Panchkula. You still deal with one coordinator and get one written estimate and one GST bill from 1st VRL Packers and Movers.

How much does it cost to shift from Ahmedabad to Gandhinagar or GIFT City?

Gandhinagar is a separate district, so a move between Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar or GIFT City is priced only after the survey. Share both addresses, the floors and a rough list of goods on the 24-hour line, and the coordinator sends a written figure plus GST.

Can you shift a house inside the old city pols?

Yes. The survey checks the lane, the staircase and the nearest point where a vehicle can stand. Goods are carried by hand to a small tempo and then moved to the truck on a main road. Any long hand carry is listed on the estimate before you approve it.

Is it a problem to move around Uttarayan or Navratri?

Moves still happen, but the days around 14 January are slow and crowded, especially in the old city, and Navratri evenings bring heavy traffic near garba venues. If your date is flexible, move a few days before or after. If it is fixed, the coordinator plans an early morning slot.

What do moves from Ahmedabad to Vadodara, Rajkot or Surat cost?

All three are in the up to 500 km band. A 1 BHK costs about Rs 9,500 to 17,000, a 2 BHK about Rs 13,000 to 24,000 and a 3 BHK about Rs 17,000 to 32,000, plus GST. A car to the same cities costs about Rs 5,500 to 9,000 for a hatchback and Rs 6,500 to 10,500 for a sedan, plus GST.

How do you protect goods during an Ahmedabad summer move?

Loading starts early in the morning, wooden furniture is wrapped in corrugated sheet and stretch film and kept out of the sun, and televisions and monitors go in foam. Candles, cosmetics and medicines should travel with you rather than in the truck, since a closed vehicle gets very hot by afternoon.

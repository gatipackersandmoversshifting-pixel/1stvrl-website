Shifting in Charkhi Dadri: what we plan for

Charkhi Dadri sits in the dry south-west of Haryana, on the rail line between Rewari and Bhiwani, with roads running out to Bhiwani, Rohtak, Jhajjar, Rewari and Mahendragarh. For decades it was part of Bhiwani district, and since 2016 it has had its own district offices, courts and police lines, which brings government staff into the town on transfer. Many families in the district have members in the army or the paramilitary forces, so serving and retired soldiers form a big share of moves. The land around is sandy and largely rain-fed, with bajra, mustard and gram as the main crops, and wells and canals matter a great deal. The town has a crowded old bazaar, newer sectors and colonies on the main roads, and village houses with wide courtyards in the countryside. Summers are fierce, with dust storms in May and June, and winter nights can be close to freezing.

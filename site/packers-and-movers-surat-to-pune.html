From Surat the truck runs south on NH48 through Navsari, Valsad and Vapi into Maharashtra, then turns off before central Mumbai and passes through the Thane and Bhiwandi side towards Kalyan and Panvel. From Panvel it climbs the Western Ghats on the Mumbai-Pune Expressway past Khalapur and Lonavala and drops into Pune near Dehu Road. Some drivers take the inland road instead, via the Saputara ghat to Nashik and then south to Pune. The road distance is about 410 km, which keeps this move on the up to 500 km band, and a shared truck usually delivers in 2 to 3 days.

Most trucks leave Surat in the morning and halt for the night around the Bhiwandi or Panvel side, then climb the expressway early the next day to avoid traffic near Lonavala. The ghat section between Khalapur and Lonavala is slow for loaded trucks, and in July and August it is often covered in rain and cloud. On the inland line via Saputara and Nashik, the ghat near Saputara is narrow and drivers prefer to cross it in daylight. Expressway and highway tolls are written as a single line.

Moving into Pune: what to plan for

Pune's newer homes in Wakad, Hinjewadi, Baner, Kharadi, Wagholi and Undri are mostly gated societies with a move-in form and a booked lift, while the older parts around Kothrud, Deccan and Sadashiv Peth have low-rise buildings and tighter parking. Traffic near the IT parks and on the Nagar Road side is heavy at office hours, so the partner team in Pune plans unloading for the middle of the day or late evening. Pune is cooler and less humid than Surat, with steady rain from June to September and mild winters.

Common delivery areas are Hinjewadi, Wakad, Baner, Aundh, Pashan, Kothrud, Warje, Kharadi, Viman Nagar, Wagholi, Hadapsar, Undri, Pimple Saudagar, Chinchwad and Talegaon.

Surat to Pune packers and movers for plant, IT and campus moves

Pune's factories and IT parks draw a large number of people from Surat. Engineers from the Hazira units and the Sachin and Pandesara industrial estates take up posts at plants around Chakan, Talegaon, Ranjangaon and Pimpri, and software staff join firms in Hinjewadi, Magarpatta and Kharadi. Their homes in Surat are usually in Adajan, Pal, Vesu or near the Hazira road, and the typical load is a 2 BHK with a car.

Students make up the other big group. Many young people from Surat go to Pune for engineering, management or design courses, and each June and July brings bookings for a bed, a study table, a few cartons and a scooter. Parents sometimes send a fuller set of furniture for a rented flat shared by two or three students; each student's cartons are marked with a name so the unloading is simple.

How the goods are protected on the ghat climb

The climb from Khalapur to Lonavala is the hardest part of the journey for a loaded truck, with steep grades and tight braking on the way down to Pune. Heavy items such as the fridge, the washing machine and steel cupboards are loaded first against the cab wall and strapped, mattresses and sofa cushions fill gaps, and cartons are packed full so nothing slides. Furniture corners are wrapped in corrugated sheet and bubble wrap.

In the monsoon the ghat section is often wrapped in cloud and heavy rain, so cartons are sealed against damp and the truck is covered with a tarpaulin. Electronics travel in foam and boxes, the TV in a padded carton marked to stay upright, and any desktop computer goes with its cables in a labelled bag. Plants and puja items are often best carried in your own car.

Common questions

How much do packers and movers charge from Surat to Pune?

For about 410 km, a 1 BHK costs Rs 9,500 - 17,000, a 2 BHK Rs 13,000 - 24,000 and a 3 BHK Rs 17,000 - 32,000, plus 18 per cent GST. The written estimate after the survey is the final price.

How long does a Surat to Pune move take?

Usually 2 to 3 days from loading on a shared truck. A dedicated truck can reach the next day at a higher cost. The driver's number is shared on loading day so you can track the ghat climb and the arrival.

Is Pimpri-Chinchwad or Chakan priced the same as Pune?

Yes. Pimpri, Chinchwad, Talegaon, Chakan, Hinjewadi and Wagholi are on the same up to 500 km chart from Surat. Give the exact address at the survey so the time for unloading is planned correctly.

What does bike shifting from Surat to Pune cost?

Roughly Rs 2,500 to 4,000 for a scooter like an Activa, Rs 3,000 to 4,500 for a bike up to 150cc like a Shine or Splendor, Rs 4,000 to 6,000 for a 150cc to 350cc bike like an Apache RTR 200 and Rs 6,000 to 9,000 for a premium bike above 350cc, plus GST.

Is Surat to Pune cheaper than Surat to Delhi?

Yes. Pune is about 410 km away and on the up to 500 km band, where a 2 BHK is roughly Rs 13,000 to 24,000 plus GST. Delhi is about 1,150 km away, on the 500 to 1,500 km band, where a 2 BHK is roughly Rs 20,000 to 36,000 plus GST.

Can my goods be delivered on a weekend in Pune?

That depends on the society. Some allow moves only on working days at set hours, and others allow weekends. Check with the society office first, and the unloading is planned inside its window.

Will my GJ-05 car need new number plates in Pune?

Not straight away. A GJ-05 car can run in Maharashtra, but if it stays more than 12 months the law expects re-registration there, which needs an NOC from the Surat RTO. Keep the RC, insurance and PUC in the glovebox while it travels.

The truck takes NH48 out of Jaipur through Udaipur and Gujarat, goes past Pune, then continues south through Satara, Kolhapur, Belagavi, Hubballi, Davanagere and Tumakuru to Bengaluru. At about 2,000 km by road, this move falls in the 1,500 km plus band of our rate card.

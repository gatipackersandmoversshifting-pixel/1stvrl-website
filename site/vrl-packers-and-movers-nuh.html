Shifting in Nuh: what we plan for

Nuh is the headquarters of the district that was called Mewat until it was renamed Nuh in 2016. The town lies between the Aravalli ridges on the road from Gurugram and Sohna to Alwar, and the hills, rocky ground and scattered villages shape every move here. The Shaheed Hasan Khan Mewati Government Medical College at Nalhar, just outside town, brings doctors, nurses and staff from across Haryana, and the district offices bring officers and teachers on postings. The old town has a historic stone tank known as Chuhimal's talab, a bazaar and closely built homes, while newer houses spread along the Sohna, Alwar and Palwal roads. Many families farm or keep cattle, and others work in transport or in Gurugram. The Delhi to Mumbai Expressway now runs through the district. Water is scarce in summer, May and June are very hot, and the monsoon can briefly flood low roads.

Home shifting in Nuh through the Aravalli roads

The roads around Nuh climb and dip through rocky Aravalli country, and some village link roads are narrow with sharp turns. For home shifting in Nuh's outlying villages, the survey checks whether a full-size truck can reach the door or whether a smaller vehicle should bring goods to the main road.

Inside town, the old bazaar lanes are busy and tight, while homes on the Sohna and Alwar roads usually have room for a truck in front. The team plans the loading point at the survey and lists any extra carry on the estimate, so the price does not change on the day.

Sohna, Gurugram and other short moves

Sohna is only about 22 km from Nuh, but it lies in Gurugram district, so a move there is on the up to 500 km band, not a local or NCR rate. A 1 BHK from Nuh to Sohna is roughly Rs 9,500 to 17,000 plus GST, and a 2 BHK roughly Rs 13,000 to 24,000 plus GST.

Moves to Gurugram's high-rise societies need a gate pass and a lift slot booked in advance. The coordinator asks for the society's rules and the possession or rent papers early, so the truck is not held at the gate when it arrives from Nuh.

Summer heat, water and the monsoon

May and June in Nuh are dry and very hot, and afternoons on open roads are tough on goods and people. The partner team starts early, keeps electronics and polished furniture in shade and wraps sofas and mattresses to keep out the dust from the rocky ground.

Monsoon showers are short but heavy, and low stretches of road can flood for a few hours. For July and August moves the coordinator checks the route before the truck leaves, wraps goods in plastic under the blankets and keeps some slack in the delivery date.

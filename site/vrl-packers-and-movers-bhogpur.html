Shifting in Bhogpur: what we plan for

Bhogpur is a nagar panchayat in Jalandhar district, strung along NH 44 between Jalandhar and Pathankot. Its best-known landmark is the Bhogpur Co-operative Sugar Mill, which began crushing in the 1955-56 season and is described as the oldest co-operative mill of the region; it has since been expanded and now has its own power plant. The town also has a busy grain market, and Bhogpur Sirwal railway station on the Jalandhar to Pathankot line. DAV University is about 15 km away, and Adampur airport roughly 21 km. Homes are mostly independent houses, with shops and flats along the highway and quieter colonies behind them. Villages such as Behram, Machrowal, Gehlar and Tandi lie around the town, and many families here have relatives abroad, so moves often mix local storage with long-distance shifting. Summers are hot and humid, and winter fog settles over the highway.

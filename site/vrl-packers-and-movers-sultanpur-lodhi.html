Shifting in Sultanpur Lodhi: what we plan for

Sultanpur Lodhi is one of the most important places in the life of Guru Nanak Dev. He spent around fourteen years here, working as the keeper of stores for Nawab Daulat Khan Lodhi, and it was in the Kali Bein that he disappeared for three days, as tradition holds, before beginning his mission. Gurdwara Ber Sahib stands on the bank of the rivulet beside an old ber tree, and Gurdwara Sant Ghat, Gurdwara Hatt Sahib and the gurdwara of Bebe Nanaki are close by. The Kali Bein itself, once badly polluted, was cleaned through kar sewa led by Sant Balbir Singh Seechewal. The town lies not far from the meeting of the Beas and the Sutlej, and its old Quila Sarai now houses the police station. Sultanpur Lodhi has a railway station, a regional campus of Guru Nanak Dev University nearby, and a mix of old houses in the lanes near the gurdwaras and newer homes on the Kapurthala and Talwandi Chaudhrian roads.

Mand villages and the Beas in the monsoon

The low-lying mand villages between Sultanpur Lodhi and the Beas have flooded in heavy monsoon years, and link roads there can stay under water or turn soft for days.

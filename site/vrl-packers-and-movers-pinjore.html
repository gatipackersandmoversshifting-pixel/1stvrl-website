Large family homes and old furniture

Many Pinjore homes have solid wood furniture that has been in the family for years: heavy beds, sheesham almirahs and dining sets. These are dismantled where possible, wrapped in blankets and stretch film, and loaded at the front of the truck so nothing heavy sits on them.

Gardens and terraces are common here too. Pots, garden tools and outdoor furniture can travel if they are listed at the survey; soil-filled pots are packed in crates so they do not spill on other goods.

Moves towards Baddi and the hills

Pinjore sits at the foot of the hills, and some families move between Pinjore and the industrial towns across the Himachal border, or up towards Solan and Shimla. These short intercity moves are priced on the up to 500 km band, with any hand carry at the hill end shown separately.

We also handle the reverse: staff coming down from the hills to settle in Pinjore or Panchkula. The truck is sized for the hill road, and the crew at the Pinjore end unloads and fits the furniture.

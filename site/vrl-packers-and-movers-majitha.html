Shifting in Majitha: what we plan for

Majitha is a sub-division town of Amritsar district, best known as the home of the Majithia sardars who served as generals in Maharaja Ranjit Singh's army. Dyal Singh Majithia, who helped found The Tribune, and Sundar Singh Majithia, who helped found Khalsa College, Amritsar, came from these families, and the town had a municipal committee as early as 1924. The main road to the Amritsar bypass is named after the freedom fighter Ram Singh Majitha and has been rebuilt in recent years, and Majitha also has a station on the Amritsar to Dera Baba Nanak railway line. Over the last fifteen years many farming families from nearby villages have built new houses in fresh colonies around the town while still looking after their fields. Older homes stand in the lanes near the bus stand, and a newer girls' school stands on Dadupura Road. Villages such as Kalera, Jethuwal, Galowali, Patalpuri and Jethunangal lie close by. Summers are hot, and fog is common in winter.

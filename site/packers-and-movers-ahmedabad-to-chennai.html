NH48 links Ahmedabad and Chennai almost end to end. The truck runs south through Vadodara, Surat and Vapi, skirts Mumbai and climbs to Pune, then continues down the Deccan through Satara, Kolhapur, Belagavi, Hubballi, Davangere, Chitradurga and Tumakuru to Bengaluru. From there it heads east through Hosur, Krishnagiri and Vellore into Chennai. The total is about 1,850 km, so this move is in the 1,500 km plus band, the top band of our rate card. A shared truck usually takes 6 to 8 days from loading.

With about 1,850 km to cover, the driver usually makes three night halts, often around Pune, near Belagavi or Hubballi, and around Bengaluru. The ghat sections between Pune and Satara get very heavy rain in the south-west monsoon, and the run through Karnataka has long open stretches between towns. Traffic around Bengaluru and Hosur is heavy, so the truck plans to pass it at night and enter Chennai in the early morning. NH48 tolls are listed together on the estimate.

Moving into Chennai: what to plan for

Chennai is hot and humid for most of the year, and unlike Ahmedabad its main rains come with the northeast monsoon from October to December, when low-lying areas such as Velachery, Pallikaranai and parts of OMR can waterlog quickly. Apartment complexes along OMR, in Sholinganallur, Perungudi, Porur and Medavakkam ask for an association form and a booked lift. Older homes in Mylapore, T Nagar and Anna Nagar may be in smaller buildings with tight stairs and limited parking for the truck, so the last stretch is sometimes done in a smaller vehicle.

Common delivery areas are Sholinganallur, Perungudi, Thoraipakkam, Siruseri, Velachery, Adyar, Mylapore, T Nagar, Anna Nagar, Porur, Guindy, Medavakkam, Tambaram and Sriperumbudur.

Our tip for this route: Between October and December, keep an eye on the weather bulletins for Tamil Nadu in the week before delivery. When a depression or cyclone is expected near the coast, we park the loaded truck somewhere safe, usually on the Bengaluru side, and bring it into Chennai only after the rain eases and the water drains.

Who moves from Ahmedabad to Chennai

Chennai draws people from Ahmedabad in a few clear ways. IT and back-office staff move to campuses on OMR, in Siruseri and around Guindy. Engineers from the Sanand car plants and the component makers around Changodar find roles in the automobile belt at Sriperumbudur and Oragadam. Many Tamil families who spent years in Ahmedabad also go back as children reach college age or parents need care, and they usually bring a full house.

Home shifting from Ahmedabad to Chennai works best when the new address and the move-in date are fixed first. A truck that reaches a Chennai complex with no lift slot booked can wait half a day at the gate. The coordinator confirms the slot with the association before the truck leaves Ahmedabad, and the Chennai partner team calls you the day before unloading.

Planning an 1,850 km move

A move of this length needs more planning than a trip within Gujarat. Book two to three weeks ahead, fix the Chennai address before the survey and carry a bag of essentials for the first week, since the goods arrive several days after you do if you fly. If you leave Ahmedabad before loading, give a trusted person the keys and a copy of the inventory sheet so the packing can go ahead on time.

Furniture with glass, marble table tops and large mirrors is crated or packed between boards for this distance, because the truck runs over the ghat sections between Pune and Kolhapur and on long stretches of open highway. Electronics are packed in foam and their serial numbers noted on the inventory sheet. If the Chennai home is not ready, storage can be arranged through a partner warehouse, charged separately and shown on the estimate.

Common questions

How much do packers and movers charge from Ahmedabad to Chennai?

For about 1,850 km, a 1 BHK costs Rs 20,000 - 35,000, a 2 BHK Rs 27,000 - 47,000 and a 3 BHK Rs 34,000 - 60,000, plus 18 per cent GST. The written estimate after the survey is the final price.

How long does shifting from Ahmedabad to Chennai take?

Plan for 6 to 8 days on a shared truck from loading. Heavy rain in the Western Ghats section in July and August, or the northeast monsoon in Tamil Nadu from October to December, can slow the trip, and you are told early if the date moves.

What does a Royal Enfield or a superbike cost to Chennai?

For this distance, a 150cc to 350cc motorcycle such as a Classic 350 is about Rs 7,000 to 10,000 plus GST, and a premium bike above 350cc about Rs 10,000 to 14,500 plus GST. A superbike goes in a wooden crate for about Rs 18,000 to 28,000 plus GST.

Will furniture from dry Ahmedabad crack or swell in Chennai?

Solid wood can move a little when it goes from dry air to sea air, which is why polished pieces are wrapped tight and unwrapped indoors, not in the open. Give the furniture a few days in a ventilated room before pushing it against a wall, and wipe off any damp with a dry cloth.

Is there a good time of year to move to Chennai?

January to March is usually the easiest, with dry roads on the whole route and milder heat in Chennai. July and August bring heavy rain in the Western Ghats, and October to December brings the northeast monsoon to Chennai, so the packing changes with the season.

We are keeping our Ahmedabad flat. Can you send only a few rooms?

Yes. Many families keep a base in Gujarat and send only one or two rooms to Chennai. The surveyor walks through with you, tags what goes, and the estimate is priced on that volume alone, usually on a shared truck.

What if the Chennai lane is too narrow for the truck?

The goods move to a smaller vehicle at the main road and are taken to the door. This transfer is shown on the estimate once the address is checked, so there is no surprise on delivery day.

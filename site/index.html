Know the cost

What decides a moving quote

A moving estimate is built from a small number of fixed factors. Understanding them helps you compare quotes on the same basis.

Road distance between the two addresses

On intercity routes, distance decides fuel and the driving hours. A move from Zirakpur to Delhi is priced differently from a move from Zirakpur to Mumbai. The coordinator gives the route distance and the transit time together.

The volume of goods

Household moves are counted by home size. A 1 BHK fits a smaller vehicle than a 3 BHK. The truck size follows the volume, and the volume follows a survey or a description of the goods on the call.

Floor and lift access

Carrying goods up a staircase takes longer than using a lift. The floor at both the pickup and the drop address is listed in the estimate, and any long carry is priced as a separate line item.

The packing grade

Basic packing uses cartons and tape. Full packing adds bubble wrap, stretch film and moving blankets for furniture. The grade is chosen on the call and listed in the estimate.

The move date

Weekends and the peak months cost more than a mid-week move in a quiet month. The date is confirmed before the estimate is final.

Once these factors are fixed, the estimate is written and shared with you. The number does not change after the truck is sealed unless the volume or the access changes and is confirmed with you first.

The estimate is not a verbal range. It is a written figure with the packing, transport, insurance and any floor work listed as separate lines, so you can see exactly what each part of the move costs.

Packing materials

What the crew brings to the door

  • Cardboard cartons in three sizes for kitchen, books and clothes.
  • Bubble wrap for glassware, crockery and fragile items.
  • Stretch film for sofas, mattresses and upholstered furniture.
  • Moving blankets to cover wooden furniture against scratches.
  • Packing tape, labels and markers for the inventory.
  • Dismantling tools and re-assembly tools.

The materials are included in the estimate and are brought by the crew. You do not need to buy them separately. High-value items such as televisions and computer monitors are packed with extra padding and listed on the inventory.

Moving tips

Things that make a move easier

  • Keep important documents, cash and jewellery with you, not in the truck.
  • Defrost the fridge a day before the move and empty the washing machine.
  • Take photos of electronics wiring before they are packed.
  • Label the boxes that need to open first at the new home.
  • Keep the essentials bag separate with clothes and toiletries for a day or two.
  • Clear the society gate pass and lift booking a day in advance.
  • Note down the driver and coordinator contact before the truck leaves.
Documents

What you may be asked for

Most local moves need no paperwork beyond the booking details. On intercity and vehicle moves, the crew or the coordinator may ask for the following.

  • Photo ID for the person booking the move.
  • Vehicle documents for car and bike transport.
  • Society gate pass or NOC where the housing society requires one.
  • A delivery address and a contact at the drop location.

The documents are checked before the vehicle leaves, not on the day of delivery. This keeps the move moving on schedule.

The move day

What happens when the crew arrives

The moving day runs in a fixed order. The crew arrives at the pickup address, walks through the goods with you, and starts packing room by room.

Packing

The kitchen is packed first, followed by bedrooms and the living room. Fragile items are wrapped separately and listed on the inventory. Large furniture is dismantled where it needs to be.

Loading

Heavy items go into the vehicle first and lighter boxes on top. The load is secured so it does not shift on the road. The vehicle is then sealed and the driver contact is shared with you.

Unloading and re-assembly

At the drop address the crew unloads in the reverse order and re-assembles the furniture. The goods are counted against the inventory and the delivery is closed with you.

The time for each step depends on the volume. A 1 BHK finishes faster than a 3 BHK. The coordinator gives a realistic time for your move on the call.

Comparing movers

How to read a moving quote

Two quotes for the same move can look different when they are not built on the same basis. Check the following points before you compare.

  • Is the estimate written, or given verbally on the phone.
  • Does it list the packing grade and the truck size.
  • Is transit insurance included or listed separately.
  • Are floor charges and long carry listed as line items.
  • Does the transit time match the route distance.

A written estimate that lists these points is easier to compare than a single number. 1st VRL Packers and Movers sends the estimate in writing before anything is loaded.

Transit insurance is available for vehicles and is listed in the estimate. The transit time follows the distance and the driving hours on the route.

Before the move day

A short checklist for the day before

  • Defrost the refrigerator and remove the water tray.
  • Drain the washing machine and disconnect it.
  • Take photos of television and home theatre wiring.
  • Separate the items you will carry yourself, including documents and valuables.
  • Clear the society gate pass and lift booking if required.
  • Keep a bag with clothes and toiletries for the first day at the new home.
  • Confirm the pickup time and the crew count with the coordinator.

These steps take under an hour and prevent most delays on the moving day. The crew handles the packing, the loading and the transport once they arrive.

Storage when needed

Goods that wait between two homes

When the new home is not ready on the move date, the goods can be held in a covered, pallet-raised warehouse with restricted access. The inventory is recorded at the time of storage.

  • Short holds when possession dates do not line up.
  • Month to month storage for longer needs.
  • Covered floor with goods kept off the ground.
  • Delivery to the new address when it is ready.

Storage is priced by volume and duration. The coordinator gives a written figure before the goods are stored.

What we do not move

Items to keep with you

A few items are not carried on the truck for safety and legal reasons. These should stay with you during the move.

  • Cash, jewellery and important documents.
  • Firearms and hazardous or flammable materials.
  • Gas cylinders and pressurised containers.
  • Live plants and pets, unless arranged separately.
  • Perishable food items.

If you are unsure whether an item can be moved, ask the coordinator on the call. It is better to confirm before the crew arrives than to hold the truck on the day.

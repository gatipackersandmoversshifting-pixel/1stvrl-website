Shifting in Dharuhera: what we plan for

Dharuhera grew from a village on the Delhi to Jaipur highway into an industrial town. The HSIIDC industrial area and large plants, including the Hero MotoCorp factory, employ thousands of engineers, supervisors and workers from many states, and Bhiwadi's industry across the Rajasthan border is only a few kilometres away. Housing is a mix: the old Dharuhera village with tight lanes, colonies of rented rooms near the factories, and newer housing societies and plotted townships along the highway and the Sohna road. Traffic on NH48 runs heavy day and night, with container trucks, buses and factory transport. The Sahibi river and the Masani barrage lie nearby, and after heavy monsoon rain the highway near Dharuhera is known for waterlogging that can slow traffic for hours. Summers are hot and dry, and December fog thickens over the open fields.

Loading on the NH48 service road

Houses and societies near the highway are reached from the service road, which carries its own stream of trucks, autos and two-wheelers. The partner team parks on the service lane with a helper at the rear, and long items such as beds and wardrobes are carried across only when the lane is clear.

Monsoon waterlogging and timing

After a heavy downpour, the stretch of NH48 near Dharuhera can flood, and traffic sometimes crawls for hours. For moves in July and August the coordinator checks the road before the truck sets out, keeps furniture wrapped in plastic under the blanket layer and allows a spare day for long trips.

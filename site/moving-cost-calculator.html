What changes the price

Volume and vehicle

  • A 2 BHK uses 25 to 35 cartons and a 17 ft truck
  • An oversized vehicle bills as a half load
  • An undersized vehicle needs a second trip

Access at both ends

  • Stair carry costs Rs 800 to Rs 2,000 per floor
  • Narrow lanes need a shuttle at Rs 1,000 to Rs 2,500
  • Society gate pass and lift booking 48 hours ahead

Timing

  • Mid-month weekdays cost 15 to 25 per cent less
  • Month end and festivals carry a premium
  • Monsoon adds waterproof packing at Rs 1,000 to Rs 2,500

Quick answers

How accurate is this calculator?

It gives the same band our coordinators quote over the phone. The exact figure depends on the inventory, the floor access and the date, and it comes from a free 15 minute survey.

Does the price include GST?

No. GST at 18 per cent is extra on a full service move. A transport-only booking under a goods transport agency can attract 5 per cent under reverse charge.

What if my move is not in these categories?

Call +91 82640 91243. Industrial shifting, international relocation and partial moves are quoted per job because the equipment and the destination change the cost structure.

Is transit insurance included in the band?

Basic handling protection is included. Full cover at 2 per cent of the declared value is a separate line and the calculator adds it when you select it.

Do I have to book after using the calculator?

No. The calculator is free and does not commit you to anything. Use it to plan a budget, then call or WhatsApp when you are ready.

Shifting in Julana: what we plan for

Julana is a municipal committee town and tehsil headquarters in Jind district, the district often called the heart of Haryana. It lies on the highway between Jind and Rohtak and has a railway station on the Rohtak to Jind line, with buses running to Delhi, Gurugram and Jind. The town has two grain markets, an old one and a new one built to ease the traffic jams of the kharif season, and a vegetable market on the highway. Shri Sanatan Dharam Sanskrit Mahavidyalaya is a well-known Sanskrit college here, and a government college runs on Karsola Road. The tehsil has about thirty villages, including Lajwana Kalan, Karsola, Karela, Malvi, Nandgarh, Shadipur, Desh Khera and Kila Zafargarh. Homes are mostly independent houses, from older ones near the bazaar to newer houses on the highway and the college road. Many residents commute to Rohtak, Delhi and Gurugram for work. Summers are hot and dry, and fog is common on the highway in winter.

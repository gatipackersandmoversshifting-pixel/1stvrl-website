The truck leaves Indore on the Ahmedabad road towards Betma and follows NH47 west across the Malwa plateau past Ghatabillod, the Dhar turn-off and Sardarpur. It then crosses the hilly tribal belt around Jhabua and enters Gujarat at Dahod, continuing through Godhra and the Panchmahal district to Ahmedabad. The road distance is about 395 km, so this move is on the up to 500 km band of our chart, and household goods usually arrive in 1 to 2 days.

This is a one-day drive with no night halt on a dedicated truck. NH47 runs over open Malwa farmland past the Dhar turn-off and then through hilly, wooded country around Jhabua and the Gujarat border at Dahod, where curves and slopes slow loaded trucks. In July and August, the hill section can be wet and misty. Tolls are added up and shown as a single line on the estimate.

Moving into Ahmedabad: what to plan for

Ahmedabad homes vary a lot. Families from Indore often move into apartment societies in Bopal, South Bopal, Prahlad Nagar, Satellite, Thaltej, Gota or Chandkheda, where the society office asks for a form and fixes shifting hours. The old walled city has pols with lanes too narrow for a truck, much like the lanes around Rajwada. Summers are very hot, often hotter than in Indore, and the rain falls mostly in July and August. A verified partner team in Ahmedabad handles the unloading.

Common delivery areas are Bopal, South Bopal, Prahlad Nagar, Satellite, Vastrapur, Thaltej, Bodakdev, SG Highway, Gota, Chandkheda, Motera, Navrangpura, Maninagar, Naroda and Gandhinagar.

Our tip for this route: Ahmedabad summer afternoons can be harsh for both people and polished furniture. If you are moving in April or May, ask for an early morning unloading slot at the new society.

Indore to Ahmedabad packers and movers for business families

Trade has linked Indore and Ahmedabad for generations, and many moves on this route are business families shifting a branch, a godown office or the whole household. Others are engineers and managers from the chemical, textile and engineering units around Ahmedabad, staff joining finance firms in GIFT City, and students going to colleges in the city. Some families return to Gujarat after years in Indore and bring a full home with them.

Because the trip is short, many customers ask whether a cheaper local truck is enough. A move of nearly 400 km still needs full packing, a numbered inventory and a vehicle sized to the load, and those are what the written estimate covers.

A short highway trip, packed properly

NH47 has hilly stretches around Jhabua and Dahod, with curves that shake loose any badly stacked load. Heavy items go at the bottom near the cabin, cartons are packed tight around them, and furniture is tied to the truck walls. Mattresses are wrapped in corrugated sheet or plastic and sofas are covered fully against dust.

Office moves on this route are often done over a weekend: packing on Friday evening, travel overnight and setting up on Saturday. A small office of up to 10 workstations costs roughly Rs 25,000 to 45,000 plus GST, and a medium office of 10 to 30 workstations roughly Rs 50,000 to 1,00,000 plus GST.

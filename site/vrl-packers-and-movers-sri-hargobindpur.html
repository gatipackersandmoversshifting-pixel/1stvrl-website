Shifting in Sri Hargobindpur: what we plan for

Sri Hargobindpur sits on the bank of the Beas river, at the edge of Gurdaspur district where it faces Hoshiarpur district across the water. The town traces its history to Guru Arjan, who bought the land, and Guru Hargobind, who founded the settlement, and it was later a seat of the Ramgarhia Misl. Gurdwara Damdama Sahib marks the site of a 1630 battle, and Guru ki Maseet, built by Guru Hargobind for the local Muslim community, is the oldest monument still standing. It is a small municipal town, so most moves are families going to Batala, Amritsar or Jalandhar, or coming home after retirement. The countryside is fertile farmland under wheat, paddy and sugarcane, and the river belt brings its own challenges: soft riverside tracks, high water in the monsoon and damp winters. Homes are mostly older brick houses in the town and larger farm houses in the surrounding villages.

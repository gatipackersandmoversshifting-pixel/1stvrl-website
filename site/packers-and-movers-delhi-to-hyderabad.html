Delhi to Hyderabad at a glance

1,580 kmRoad distance, approx.
6 to 8 daysHousehold transit time
NH44Main route
1,500 km plusPrice band used below

The truck runs south on NH44 through Agra, Gwalior, Sagar and Nagpur. At about 1,580 km this route falls in the 1,500 km plus price band. Household goods usually reach in 6 to 8 days on a shared truck.

The truck halts overnight near Gwalior or Jhansi and again near Nagpur, then runs down NH44 through Adilabad into Telangana. Much of the Nagpur to Hyderabad stretch is fast highway, so the last day of the trip is usually smooth. Toll charges on NH44 are shown as one line in the estimate.

Moving into Hyderabad: what to plan for

A large share of moves into Hyderabad end in gated communities in Gachibowli, Kondapur, Madhapur, Kokapet and Kukatpally. These usually need a move-in slot, a lift booking and a gate pass from the association, and some allow trucks only at set hours.

Common delivery areas are Gachibowli, Kondapur, Madhapur, HITEC City, Kokapet, Narsingi, Kukatpally, Miyapur, Manikonda and Secunderabad.

Our tip for this route: Book your community's move-in slot as soon as the delivery date is fixed. A truck that waits at the gate adds waiting charges.

Shifting in Dera Baba Nanak: what we plan for

Dera Baba Nanak stands near the Ravi river on the international border, at the Indian end of the Kartarpur Corridor, which opened in November 2019 and lets pilgrims cross to Gurdwara Darbar Sahib Kartarpur in Pakistan. The town itself is a major pilgrimage centre, with Gurdwara Sri Chola Sahib and Gurdwara Darbar Sahib drawing large crowds, especially around Guru Nanak's gurpurab. The corridor brought a passenger terminal, a wide approach road and new hotels and shops. Away from the town, the country is flat farmland under wheat, paddy and sugarcane, with border villages spread along narrow link roads. Many families have members serving in the forces or living abroad. Homes range from old brick houses in the town centre to new kothis on the Batala road. The Ravi has flooded villages in heavy monsoon years, and winter fog can be very dense.

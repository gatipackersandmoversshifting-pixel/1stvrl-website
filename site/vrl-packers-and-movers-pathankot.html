Shifting in Pathankot: what we plan for

Pathankot sits where Punjab meets Jammu and Kashmir and Himachal Pradesh. NH44 runs north from here towards Jammu, the road to Dalhousie and Dharamshala climbs east into the hills, and the Kangra Valley narrow-gauge railway starts at Pathankot. The Mamun cantonment and the Air Force station make service transfers a large share of moves. Civilian homes are spread across the town, along the Dhangu, Dalhousie and Mission roads, and towards Sujanpur and Madhopur. Summer is hot, and the monsoon in July and August is heavy across the foothills.

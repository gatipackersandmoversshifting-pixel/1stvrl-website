Shifting in Shahkot: what we plan for

Shahkot is the headquarters of a tehsil of about 176 villages in the south-west corner of Jalandhar district, and it sits on NH703, the road that runs from Jalandhar through Moga and Barnala towards Sirsa. Just south of the town flows the Sutlej, the river that separates Doaba from Malwa, with Moga district on the far bank. A long earthen embankment, the dhussi bundh, guards the low fields along the river. The town works as a trading and service centre for villages such as Bahmanian, Malsian, Kotla Surajmal and Talwandi Madho, with shops, grain traders, workshops and coaching centres along the highway. Many families have relatives abroad, and big kothis stand next to older brick houses. Farming here is wheat and paddy, the summers are hot and humid, and the winter months bring thick fog across the river plain.

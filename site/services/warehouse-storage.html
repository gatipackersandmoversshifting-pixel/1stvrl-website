What is included in storage

  • Covered floor with goods kept off the ground on pallets.
  • Restricted access to the storage area.
  • Inventory of the goods at the time of storage.
  • Short holds when possession dates do not line up.
  • Month to month storage for longer needs.

When storage helps

Storage works when the new home is not ready on the move date, or when a tenant moves out before the next place is confirmed. The goods wait in the warehouse and are delivered when the drop address is ready.

How storage runs

Pickup and inventory

The crew collects the goods and lists them on an inventory. The items are packed and moved to the warehouse.

Storage

The goods are kept on pallets off the floor in a covered area with restricted access. The inventory stays with the records.

Delivery

When the drop address is ready, the goods are loaded and delivered. The inventory is checked again at delivery.

What affects the cost

  • The volume of goods stored.
  • The duration of storage, usually per month.
  • The pickup and delivery distances.

Documents and booking

Storage is booked with the volume and the likely duration. The coordinator gives a written figure before the goods are stored, and the inventory is recorded at pickup.

  • Photo ID for the person booking the storage.
  • The list of goods to be stored.
  • A contact for when the goods are delivered.

Tips for smoother storage

  • Keep the inventory list with you for reference.
  • Store only dry, clean goods to avoid moisture damage.
  • Note the delivery date so the goods are released on time.

Frequently asked questions

How is the storage priced?

Storage is priced by the volume of goods and the duration, usually per month. The coordinator gives a written figure before the goods are stored.

Is the storage area secure?

Yes. The area has restricted access and the goods are listed on an inventory at the time of storage.

Can I store for a short period?

Yes. Short holds are available when possession dates do not line up.

Do you deliver after storage?

Yes. The goods are delivered to the new address when it is ready, with the inventory checked again.

How do I book storage?

Call +91 82640 91243 with the volume and the likely duration. A written estimate follows.

Can I access my goods during storage?

Access is arranged with the coordinator and noted in the records. The area has restricted access and the goods are listed on an inventory.

Do you store office goods too?

Yes. Office furniture, files and equipment can be stored the same way as household goods.

What if I need storage for several months?

Month to month storage is available for longer needs. The coordinator gives a written figure for the duration before the goods are stored.

Are the goods covered while stored?

The goods are listed on an inventory at pickup and kept on pallets off the floor. Insurance can be discussed for the storage period.

Can I store a vehicle too?

Vehicles are handled separately through car and bike transport. Speak to the coordinator about combined needs.

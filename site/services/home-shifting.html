What is included in home shifting

  • Packing of all household goods with cartons, wrap and moving blankets.
  • Dismantling of beds, wardrobes and other large furniture.
  • Loading into the truck, with the vehicle sealed at the start.
  • Transport to the new address on the agreed route.
  • Unloading and re-assembly of furniture at the drop location.

How the crew works

The team arrives with packing materials and tools. Fragile and high-value items are packed separately and listed on the inventory. The driver contact is shared with you, and the arrival time is given against the distance and permitted driving hours.

Room by room packing

Packing follows a fixed order so nothing is missed. The kitchen is packed first because it holds the most breakables. Bedrooms come next, then the living room, and large furniture is dismantled last.

Kitchen

Glassware, crockery and utensils are wrapped in bubble wrap and packed in labelled cartons. Appliances are emptied, cleaned and wrapped separately.

Bedrooms

Clothes go into cartons, mattresses are wrapped in stretch film, and beds are dismantled. Wardrobes are emptied and the mirrors are padded.

Living room

Televisions and electronics are packed with extra padding and photographed before packing. Sofas and upholstered furniture are wrapped in stretch film.

Large furniture

Beds, wardrobes and dining tables are dismantled where possible. Moving blankets cover the wooden surfaces to avoid scratches during the journey.

What affects the cost

  • Road distance between the pickup and drop address.
  • The volume of goods, counted as a 1 BHK, 2 BHK, 3 BHK or larger load.
  • Floor and lift access at both locations.
  • The packing grade, from basic cartons to full furniture wrapping.
  • The move date, with weekend and peak-season slots priced clearly.

Before the move day

  • Defrost the refrigerator and empty the washing machine.
  • Keep cash, jewellery and documents with you.
  • Separate the items you will carry yourself.
  • Clear the society gate pass and lift booking if required.

Documents and booking

A local home shift needs no paperwork beyond the booking details. On intercity moves the crew may ask for a photo ID and, where the society requires it, a gate pass or NOC. The documents are checked before the vehicle leaves, not on the day of delivery.

  • Photo ID for the person booking the move.
  • Society gate pass or NOC where required.
  • A contact at the drop address for delivery.

Tips for a smoother home shift

  • Keep an essentials bag with clothes and toiletries for a day or two.
  • Label the boxes that need to open first at the new home.
  • Take photos of electronics wiring before they are packed.
  • Confirm the gate pass and lift booking a day in advance.
  • Note the driver and coordinator contact before the truck leaves.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a home shift take?

A local 1 BHK shift usually finishes in a day. A 2 BHK or 3 BHK shift can take one to two days depending on the volume and the distance.

Do you provide packing materials?

Yes. Cartons, bubble wrap, stretch film and moving blankets are provided by the crew and included in the estimate.

How do I book a home shift?

Call +91 82640 91243 or fill the quote form on the contact page. A coordinator sends a written estimate before anything is loaded.

Is my furniture dismantled and re-assembled?

Yes. Beds, wardrobes and other large furniture are dismantled before loading and re-assembled at the new address.

Can you move a 4 BHK house?

Yes. Larger homes are moved with a bigger truck and a larger crew, planned over one to two days as the volume needs.

Do you empty the fridge and washing machine?

The refrigerator should be defrosted and the washing machine drained by you before the move. The crew packs them once they are empty and dry.

How do you pack fragile items?

Glassware, crockery and fragile items are wrapped in bubble wrap and packed in labelled cartons with padding. They are listed on the inventory separately.

What happens if the volume is more than estimated?

The coordinator confirms the revised figure before loading. Nothing is added after the truck is sealed without your agreement.

Is there a charge for floor and lift access?

Floor and long carry work is listed as a line item in the estimate. It is stated before the move, not added after delivery.

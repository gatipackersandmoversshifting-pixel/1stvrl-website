How an intercity move runs

The crew packs the goods at the pickup address and loads them into a truck sized for the volume. The vehicle is sealed and the driver contact is shared with you. The coordinator tracks the vehicle and gives the delivery window at the drop city.

Intercity move steps

Booking and estimate

The coordinator notes the two cities, the volume and the packing grade, then sends a written estimate with the route distance and the transit window.

Packing and loading

The crew packs and loads on the agreed date. The vehicle is sealed at loading and the driver contact is shared.

Transit

The coordinator tracks the vehicle on the route and shares updates. The arrival window is given against the distance and driving hours.

Delivery

The goods are unloaded at the drop address and the inventory is checked. The move is closed with you.

What affects the cost

  • Road distance between the two cities.
  • The volume of goods and the truck size.
  • The packing grade and the number of fragile items.
  • Floor and lift access at both addresses.
  • Transit insurance, which is listed separately in the estimate.

Documents and booking

An intercity move is booked a week to ten days ahead, and earlier in the peak season. The coordinator confirms the route distance, the truck size and the transit window before the estimate is final.

  • Photo ID for the person booking the move.
  • Vehicle documents if a car or bike is moving too.
  • A contact at the drop city for delivery.

Tips for a smoother intercity move

  • Book early in the peak season to lock the date.
  • Keep important documents and valuables with you.
  • Label the boxes that need to open first at the drop.
  • Confirm the transit insurance before the vehicle leaves.

Frequently asked questions

How long does an intercity move take?

The transit time depends on the distance and the driving hours on the route. The coordinator gives the arrival window when the booking is made.

Is transit insurance included?

Transit insurance is available and listed in the estimate. It is not hidden inside the base price.

Is the truck shared with other customers?

The truck is booked for your move and sealed at loading. It is not shared with other consignments.

Can I track the vehicle during transit?

Yes. The coordinator tracks the vehicle and shares updates on the route.

How do I book an intercity move?

Call +91 82640 91243 with the pickup and drop city. A written estimate follows before anything is loaded.

Which cities do you move to?

Intercity moves run to Delhi, Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata and other cities across India on fixed routes.

Do you move cars and bikes on the same move?

Yes. Cars and bikes travel on their own carriers, not with the household goods, and can be booked with the same coordinator.

How is the truck size decided?

The truck size follows the volume of goods. A 1 BHK does not pay for a 3 BHK truck. The coordinator matches the vehicle to the load.

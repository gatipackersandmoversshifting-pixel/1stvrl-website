Frequently asked questions

How long does bike transport take?

The transit time depends on the distance and the driving hours on the route. The coordinator gives the arrival window when the booking is made.

Is the bike insured during transport?

Transit insurance is available and listed in the estimate. The bike condition is noted before loading.

What documents are needed?

The bike registration and your photo ID are needed at pickup. The documents are handed over at delivery.

Is the fuel drained before loading?

Yes. The fuel is drained for safety, and the mirrors are wrapped before the bike is loaded.

How do I book bike transport?

Call +91 82640 91243 with the pickup and drop city and the bike model. A written estimate follows.

Can I keep accessories on the bike?

It is better to remove loose accessories and a top box before loading. The mirrors are wrapped and the bike is secured on the carrier.

Is door to door delivery available?

Yes. The bike is delivered to the door at the drop city, with the pickup and drop points confirmed with you in advance.

How is the bike secured in transit?

The bike is strapped in place on the carrier so it does not move during the journey. The condition is checked again at delivery.

Do you move bikes on intercity routes?

Yes. Bikes travel on their own carrier on intercity routes and are not loaded with household goods.

Can I book a bike with a home shift?

Yes. The bike and the home goods can be booked with the same coordinator, but they travel on separate vehicles.

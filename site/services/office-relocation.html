What is included in office relocation

  • Packing of workstations, chairs, cabinets and conference furniture.
  • IT handling for computers, servers and cabling with labelled boxes.
  • Dismantling and re-assembly of office furniture.
  • Loading and transport on the agreed schedule.
  • Unloading and placement at the new office as per the floor plan.

How the move window is planned

The coordinator works with your facility team to fix a slot. The crew arrives after hours or on the weekend where needed, so desks are clear by the next working day.

IT equipment handling

Computers, monitors and servers are the sensitive part of an office move. Each workstation is labelled, the cables are photographed or tagged, and the IT team gets a packing sequence that lets them restore systems quickly.

Workstations

Monitors and CPUs are packed with padding and labelled by desk number, so the same setup lands at the right desk in the new office.

Server rooms

Servers and racks are moved with anti-static wrap and in a planned sequence with the IT team. The downtime is kept to a minimum.

Files and documents

Files are boxed by department and labelled. Sensitive records stay sealed through the move and are handed over by count.

What affects the cost

  • Number of workstations and the total volume of furniture.
  • Floor and lift access at both offices.
  • IT equipment and server handling.
  • The distance between the old and new office.
  • The move window, including after-hours and weekend slots.

Documents and booking

An office move is planned around the business. The coordinator confirms the move window with the facility team and notes any building access rules for the crew. For larger offices a survey is scheduled before the estimate.

  • Number of workstations and the two addresses.
  • Building access rules for the crew and the truck.
  • The IT team contact for server and network handling.

Tips for a smoother office move

  • Ask staff to clear their desks and label personal items.
  • Back up servers and data before the move day.
  • Share the new floor plan so the crew places furniture correctly.
  • Keep a contact available on the move day for quick decisions.

Frequently asked questions

Can you move a server room?

Yes. Servers and racks are packed with anti-static wrap and moved in a planned sequence with the IT team.

Do you work after office hours?

Yes. After-hours and weekend moves are available so the business stays open during the day.

How do you label the equipment?

Each workstation is labelled by desk number and the cables are tagged, so the same setup is restored at the new office.

Can you move confidential records?

Yes. Files are boxed by department, sealed and handed over by count. The boxes stay closed through the move.

How do I get a quote?

Call +91 82640 91243 with the number of workstations and the two addresses. A written estimate follows.

How much notice do you need?

A week is enough for a small office. Larger offices are better planned two weeks ahead so the survey and the move window can be fixed.

Do you move conference tables and cabins?

Yes. Conference tables, cabins and partition furniture are dismantled, moved and re-assembled at the new office.

What happens if the move runs over the window?

The coordinator keeps the facility team informed. The crew works until the job is complete and the overtime, if any, is confirmed with you first.

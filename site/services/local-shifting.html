How a local shift runs

The crew arrives at the pickup address, packs the goods, loads the vehicle and moves to the drop address in the same day. The vehicle is sealed at loading and the driver contact is shared with you.

Local shift steps

Survey or call

The coordinator notes the volume, the floor and the distance on the call. A written estimate follows before the move is confirmed.

Packing and loading

The crew packs the goods, loads the vehicle and seals it. The packing follows the same room by room order as a full home shift.

Unloading

The goods are unloaded at the drop address and placed in the rooms. The move is closed with the inventory check.

What affects the cost

  • The distance between the pickup and drop address.
  • The volume of goods.
  • The floor and lift access at both ends.
  • The packing grade.
  • Any long carry from the vehicle to the door.

Documents and booking

A local shift needs no paperwork beyond the booking details. Where the housing society requires it, a gate pass or lift booking is arranged a day ahead. The coordinator confirms the access on the call.

  • Society gate pass or lift booking where required.
  • A contact at the drop address for delivery.

Tips for a smoother local shift

  • Keep cash, jewellery and documents with you.
  • Pack a small essentials bag for the first day.
  • Clear the parking for the vehicle before the crew arrives.
  • Confirm the entry timing with the society office.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a local shift take?

A local 1 BHK shift usually finishes in a day. A larger home can take a full day or part of the next day depending on the volume.

Do you cover Chandigarh and Mohali?

Yes. Chandigarh and Mohali moves are handled on request with the same crew and vehicle.

Can I move just a few items?

Yes. A few items within the city are covered under the minimum charge. The coordinator gives the figure on the call.

Is there a floor charge?

Floor and long carry work is listed as a line item in the estimate. It is stated before the move.

How do I book a local shift?

Call +91 82640 91243 or fill the quote form. A written estimate follows before anything is loaded.

How many crew members arrive?

The crew size follows the volume. A 1 BHK shift uses a smaller team than a 3 BHK shift. The coordinator confirms the crew count on the call.

Do you work on weekends?

Yes. Weekend moves are available and are listed in the estimate. Weekends and peak months are priced clearly.

What if my society has an entry timing?

Share the society entry timing on the call. The crew and the vehicle are scheduled around it so there is no wait at the gate.

Do you dismantle furniture on local shifts?

Yes. Beds and wardrobes are dismantled and re-assembled on local shifts the same way as a full home move.

Can I get a same-day local move?

Same-day moves depend on availability. Call early and the coordinator will confirm whether the slot is open.

How the move is planned

A survey lists the goods and the destination. The coordinator prepares the customs documents and the shipping option, then gives a written estimate that lists packing, freight and insurance.

International move steps

Survey and valuation

The goods are listed and valued for customs. The destination country decides the documents and the packing standard.

Export packing

Goods are packed with custom crates and export-grade wrapping. The inventory is prepared for customs.

Shipment

The goods ship by sea or air as the job and the budget decide. The freight is listed in the estimate.

Destination delivery

The shipment is coordinated at the destination port and delivered to the door.

What affects the cost

  • The destination country and the port.
  • The volume of goods and the shipment type.
  • Sea or air freight.
  • Customs documentation and duties.

Documents and booking

An international move needs the inventory and valuation for customs, along with the destination details. The survey lists the goods first, then the documentation is prepared.

  • Inventory and valuation of the goods.
  • Passport and visa details where required.
  • The destination country and the port.

Tips for a smoother international move

  • Start the survey early, as documentation takes time.
  • Declare the goods accurately for customs.
  • Keep a copy of the inventory for the destination port.

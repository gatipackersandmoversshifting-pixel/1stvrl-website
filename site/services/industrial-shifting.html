How the move is planned

An engineer or supervisor visits the site first and notes the machine dimensions, the access and the lift requirements. The estimate follows the survey and lists the handling, transport and insurance.

Industrial move steps

Site survey

The machine dimensions, the access and the lift requirements are noted. The survey decides the trailer size and the handling gear.

Dismantling and crating

Machines are disconnected, dismantled where needed and crated. The parts are labelled so they re-assemble correctly.

Lifting and loading

Cranes and slings lift the equipment onto the trailer. The load is secured for the journey.

Placement

The equipment is placed at the new site and re-installed with the same labelling.

What affects the cost

  • The machine size and weight.
  • The lifting gear and crane requirements.
  • The distance between the two sites.
  • The dismantling and re-installation work.

Tips for a smoother industrial move

  • Disconnect power and utilities before the machinery is moved.
  • Clear the loading bay for the trailer and the crane.
  • Label the parts so they re-assemble correctly.

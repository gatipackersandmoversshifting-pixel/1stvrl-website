Shifting in Bhimtal: what we plan for

Bhimtal is built around the largest lake in the Nainital lake belt, with a small island in the middle of the water and the old Bhimeshwar Mahadev temple close to the dam. The town is more open than Nainital, and the lake road is fairly level, but many homes on the slopes towards Mehragaon, Sattal and Naukuchiatal are reached by narrow link roads and flights of steps. Bhimtal is also a campus town. Kumaun University runs its Sir J C Bose technical campus here, and the Birla Institute of Applied Sciences, a Graphic Era Hill University campus and the ICAR cold water fisheries research directorate all bring staff and students. A small industrial area on the edge of town adds factory workers, and many cottages belong to families from Delhi and the NCR. Winters are cold and frosty, and the monsoon brings slips on the road up from Kathgodam.

VRL Packers and Movers Bhimtal for campus staff and students

Teachers, researchers and administrative staff at the Bhimtal institutes usually move at the end of a semester or when a contract ends, and they often carry more books, files and lab notes than furniture. The survey counts the book cartons separately, because a wall of books in a small flat can fill a vehicle faster than a sofa set and a double bed.

Students and young staff living in a single room or a paying-guest set-up can book a 1 RK move. Inside Bhimtal or to Naukuchiatal, that is a local job of roughly Rs 3,000 to 6,500 plus GST; to Delhi, about 300 km away, it is in the up to 500 km band at roughly Rs 8,500 to 15,000 plus GST.

Cottages and second homes of NCR families

A good share of the calls from Bhimtal come from families in Delhi, Noida and Gurugram who own a cottage by the lake or on the Sattal road. Some want the furniture brought down to the city after selling the house, others want a family home emptied while they stay away. A caretaker can be present for the survey, and the written estimate is then sent to you on WhatsApp for approval.

Cottages that stay closed for months often have damp in the cupboards and dust on the woodwork. The partner team wipes items before wrapping, uses fresh cartons for linen and keeps a list of each room's contents. A 3 BHK cottage to Gurugram, about 320 km by road, is roughly Rs 17,000 to 32,000 plus GST.

Link roads, steps and smaller vehicles

The climb from Kathgodam has tight bends, and the lanes around Bhimtal and Naukuchiatal are too narrow for a full-size truck. A smaller vehicle collects the goods from the nearest point it can reach and, for long moves, they are shifted to a larger truck in the plains. The survey decides where this changeover happens and what it costs.

Where a house sits well above or below the road, the crew carries furniture and cartons by hand. Steps are counted during the survey and the hand carry is written into the estimate, so the price does not change on the day. Fragile items such as glass tops and mirrors are carried separately on these paths.

Seasons that shape a Bhimtal move

From mid-May to the end of June, holiday traffic on the lake road and the Kathgodam climb can slow a loaded vehicle to a crawl, especially on weekends. July to September is the rainy season, when slips can block the road for hours. October, November, March and April are usually the easiest months for home shifting in Bhimtal.

Winter brings frost and the occasional snowfall on the higher ridges, and the plains below can be under thick fog in December and January. The coordinator checks the weather before fixing the date, keeps a spare day for long trips and avoids night driving on the hill section.

Shifting in Chandigarh: what we plan for

Home shifting in Chandigarh follows the sector grid. Each sector is a self-contained block with its own market strip, and houses open onto quiet inner roads rather than the busy sector-dividing roads, so the truck usually waits on an inner lane a short walk from the gate. The northern sectors, roughly from 2 to 11, hold one- and two-kanal kothis with lawns, servant rooms and heavy old furniture. The southern sectors from about 32 to 47 are denser, with marla houses split into floors and Housing Board flats on three or four storeys without a lift, while Sectors 48 to 51 and 63 add group housing and newer Housing Board blocks. Outside the grid, Manimajra, Mauli Jagran, Dhanas, Burail, Maloya and Daria have tight lanes and rented rooms. May and June often cross 40 degrees, the monsoon arrives in early July with short heavy bursts, and December mornings bring fog.

Our survey in Chandigarh asks three plain questions: is it a kothi, a flat or a society tower, which floor, and where can the truck stand. For a two-kanal house in Sector 8, 9 or 10 the crew is larger and packing often starts the day before, because the rooms are many and the wood is solid. For a Housing Board flat in Sector 40, 44 or 45, beds and wardrobes are opened up first and carried down a narrow staircase by hand. In Manimajra, the Modern Housing Complex and Shivalik Enclave flats are checked for stair width and parking at the survey. In Burail, Dhanas and Mauli Jagran a small tempo carries cartons from the door to the truck on the sector road. Because the crew comes from Panchkula and not from another city, an early morning start is easy to keep.

Packers and movers in Chandigarh sectors, kothis and colonies

Packers and movers in Sector 8, 9 and 11 spend most of the day on room count. A two-kanal house can hold four bedrooms of furniture, a drawing room of carved sofas, a store full of tin trunks and a garage of tools, often collected by one family over thirty years, so the survey also marks what is being given away or sold. Sector 11 and Sector 15 sit beside the PGI campus and Panjab University, and the lanes there see a steady flow of faculty, doctors, resident doctors and research scholars moving one or two rooms at the start or end of a term or a posting.

Packers and movers in Sector 35, 38, 40 and 44 deal with smaller plots and busier markets. Marla houses here are often shared floor by floor between owners and tenants, so the plan notes whose goods are whose and who uses the common stair. The co-operative group housing societies in Sector 48, 49 and 50 have lifts but tight parking, and the society office is told the date in advance. Beyond the grid, Manimajra and Mauli Jagran on the Panchkula side, Dhanas and Maloya to the west and Burail inside Sector 45 have many rented rooms, where students and workers often need a one-room move at short notice.

Transfers, weather and papers for home shifting in Chandigarh

Chandigarh is a Union Territory and the capital of both Punjab and Haryana, so transfers drive many moves: officers of the two state governments and the UT administration, staff of the Punjab and Haryana High Court, army families linked to Western Command at Chandimandir next door, and air force families at the Chandigarh air base. Government houses usually have to be handed back by a set date, so packing is planned a day or two earlier, leaving time to clear and clean the house. The GST invoice can be raised to an employer with its GSTIN, and where a bank or PSU claim needs an IBA-approved mover, the move is arranged through our IBA-approved partner (code MUK-2068).

In May and June the crew starts at first light and keeps polished wood out of the sun. July and August showers are short but heavy, so cartons are taped twice and mattresses are bagged. From mid December to January dense fog sits on NH44 towards Ambala and Karnal, and long-distance trucks leave after it lifts. If a car with a CH number is leaving the UT for good, keep the RC, insurance and pollution papers in one folder, since re-registration in the new state later needs an NOC from the Registering and Licensing Authority in Chandigarh. Settle the final utility bills and the rent deposit before the truck leaves.

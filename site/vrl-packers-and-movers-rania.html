Shifting in Rania: what we plan for

Rania is a municipal committee town on the upper bank of the Ghaggar river in western Sirsa district, about 25 km from the Rajasthan border. Once part of the Ellenabad sub-division, it is now a sub-division of its own with a tehsil office, a police station, a hospital and a grain market. It lies on State Highway 32A, the Bhambhoor to Jiwan Nagar road, and the nearest railway stations are at Sirsa and Ellenabad. The town has a long history: it was once called Bhattian, it changed hands many times in the 18th century, and its old fort still serves as the police station. The Ottu dam on the Ghaggar is close by. Wheat, cotton, paddy and vegetables are the main crops, and most homes are independent houses with a courtyard. Villages and settlements such as Jiwan Nagar, Kariwala, Sant Nagar, Balasar, Bacher, Dhottar and Kussar lie around the town. Summers are very hot and dusty, and winter mornings bring fog.

Packers and movers Rania for farm households

Many Rania families farm land in the villages around the town and keep a house in both places. A move often means bringing beds, trunks, a cooler, a fridge and a deep freezer from the village house to the town, or the other way. The survey lists each item, and the estimate shows what goes where.

Farm tools and grain are not part of a household move, but steel trunks, quilts and utensils are, and they are packed in cartons and bags that double as padding for the furniture inside the truck. Desert coolers are drained and dried before loading, because a wet cooler pad can soak the cartons stacked next to it on a long, hot drive.

Home shifting in Rania in the heat

Western Sirsa is among the hottest parts of Haryana in May and June, and dust blows in from the dry fields. For home shifting in Rania in these months, the partner team starts at first light, seals every carton, and keeps electronics in padded cartons near the cabin end of the truck.

From December to January, fog can sit over the Ghaggar belt and the Sirsa road until late morning.

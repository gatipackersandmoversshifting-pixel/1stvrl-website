Shifting in Hyderabad: what we plan for

Hyderabad moves fall into a few clear types. On the western side, from HITEC City, Madhapur and Kondapur to Gachibowli, Nanakramguda, Kokapet and Tellapur, most homes are flats in large gated communities with service lifts, basement parking and an association that controls every move. Kukatpally, Miyapur, Nizampet and Bachupally in the north-west mix newer towers with four- and five-storey apartment blocks that often have a small lift or none. Banjara Hills and Jubilee Hills have independent houses and villas on sloping roads, with heavy furniture and many rooms to clear. The Old City around Charminar and the older parts of Secunderabad have narrow lanes and steep stairs where a big truck cannot reach the door. Distances are long as well: Kompally to Tellapur is more than 35 km, and crossing the city can take well over an hour at peak time, so the survey fixes the loading time and the route along with the item list.

We plan Hyderabad moves by side of the city. Jobs on the western corridor usually load in the morning before office traffic builds around HITEC City and Gachibowli, or in the evening once it eases. In the east, in Uppal, Nagole, LB Nagar, Dilsukhnagar and Boduppal, many moves are from independent houses and older apartments, so the crew is larger and carries more by hand. North-side moves in Alwal, Kompally and Medchal are quick for intercity trucks to reach from NH44. Heavy goods vehicles face daytime restrictions on many roads inside the city, so long-distance trucks are often loaded late in the evening or early in the morning, and in narrow lanes a smaller vehicle shuttles goods to the main truck. Summers from April to June regularly cross 40 degrees, so we avoid midday loading and keep electronics out of the sun. From July to September heavy showers can flood underpasses and low roads, so cartons are stretch-wrapped and the route is checked on the day.

Common questions

How much do packers and movers charge in Hyderabad?

Within Hyderabad a 1 RK costs Rs 3,000 to 6,500, a 1 BHK Rs 5,000 to 9,000, a 2 BHK Rs 8,000 to 15,000, a 3 BHK Rs 12,000 to 21,000 and a 4 BHK or villa Rs 17,000 to 29,000, plus GST. A building with no working lift adds a stair charge, written as a separate line on the estimate.

Is a move between Secunderabad and the Cyberabad IT areas charged as local?

Yes. Within Hyderabad city, including Secunderabad and the Cyberabad IT areas, moves use the local band, not an intercity rate. For example, a 2 BHK from Secunderabad to Gachibowli costs Rs 8,000 to 15,000 plus GST. For an address on the outer edge of the city, the survey confirms the price.

Do you have an office in Hyderabad?

No. 1st VRL Packers and Movers has no office, branch or warehouse in Hyderabad. Moves in and from Hyderabad are carried out by a verified partner team and coordinated from our head office at #705, Sector 7, Panchkula, Haryana. You deal with one coordinator and get one written estimate and one GST bill from us.

What do Hyderabad gated communities ask for before a move?

Most associations want a move-in or move-out request a few days ahead, the truck number and crew names, a lift slot and a gate pass. Many also ask for a no-dues letter for maintenance or a note from the flat owner before goods can leave. Share these rules at the survey so loading is planned around them.

Can you shift from the Old City or a house with no lift?

Yes. Tell us the lane width, the floor and the staircase at the survey. Where the truck cannot reach the door, a smaller vehicle shuttles goods to it, and any stair or shuttle charge is written on the estimate in advance.

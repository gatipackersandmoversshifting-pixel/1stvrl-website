Shifting in Gurugram: what we plan for

Moving out of a Gurugram tower is rarely about the stairs and nearly always about the rules. Most condominiums on Golf Course Extension Road, Sohna Road and the Dwarka Expressway want a move-out gate pass from the facility office, a clearance that maintenance dues are paid and a fixed slot on the service lift, and several ask for a refundable deposit that is returned after a check of the lift and lobby. Older parts of the city are different again: builder floors in Sushant Lok, South City and Palam Vihar often have a narrow staircase and a lift too small for a sofa, and the villages behind MG Road have lanes where a big truck cannot turn. In July and August heavy rain can flood stretches of NH48 and low roads within an hour, so monsoon loading is planned around the weather forecast.

Before the survey we ask for the society's move rules in writing, because they decide the date as much as the truck does. For DLF Phase 5, Golf Course Road and the Golf Course Extension towers, the partner team brings lift padding and floor sheets and works inside the slot the facility office gives. In Sushant Lok, South City and the HUDA sectors of old Gurugram, wardrobes and beds are often taken apart upstairs and carried down by hand. Around Chakkarpur, Sikanderpur and Nathupur, a small tempo shuttles cartons to a truck parked on the wider road. New Gurugram sectors along the Dwarka Expressway usually have wide approach roads but strict move hours, which we note on the plan.

Home shifting in Gurugram: a guide by area

Each part of the city brings its own kind of move. Packers and movers in DLF Phase 1 to 4 deal with a mix of independent houses and builder floors, so the crew count matters more than the truck size. In DLF Phase 5 and along Golf Course Road most homes are in tall condominium towers, where the service lift slot is the real deadline. Families leaving Sushant Lok or South City usually come from third or fourth floors with tight landings, and a large L-shaped sofa may need to be opened up at the joints before it goes down.

Packers and movers in Palam Vihar and the sectors around it often have a quieter job, with lower floors and wider roads, but parking for the truck still has to be agreed with neighbours. The newer sectors along the Dwarka Expressway and in New Gurugram are mostly gated high-rises with good roads and long walks from the tower to the gate, so trolleys are sent for cartons. Old Gurugram villages such as Chakkarpur, Sikanderpur and Nathupur house many single professionals in rented rooms, and those moves are usually one room and a bike.

Weather, timing and paperwork for a Gurugram move

The calendar changes how a Gurugram move goes. From May to mid June afternoons are very hot, so loading starts early and wooden furniture is kept away from direct sun. In July and August a heavy shower can flood stretches of NH48 and many internal roads in a short time, so cartons are sealed with extra tape, mattresses are wrapped in plastic and the truck is kept on higher ground. In December and January fog slows the highways at night, which can add a day to long routes heading north or east.

Keep a few papers ready before the packing day. The society gate pass and the lift booking come first, then a copy of your ID and the new address for the transport documents. Tenants should collect the deposit settlement from the landlord before the truck leaves, because it is hard to chase from another city. If a car is going on a carrier, keep copies of the registration certificate and insurance ready for the inventory list, and if storage is needed between homes, it can be arranged through a partner warehouse, charged separately and shown on the estimate.

Common questions

Is a move from Gurugram to Delhi charged as local?

Yes. Gurugram and Delhi are priced on the local band of the house chart, because they are treated as one metro. A 1 BHK costs about Rs 5,000 to 9,000, a 2 BHK about Rs 8,000 to 15,000 and a 3 BHK about Rs 12,000 to 21,000, plus GST. Delivery timing at the Delhi end follows the goods vehicle entry rules for that road.

What about Gurugram to Faridabad, Manesar or Sohna?

All three are also on the local band. Faridabad comes under the same metro rule as Delhi, and Manesar and Sohna are short moves inside Gurugram district. A 2 BHK costs about Rs 8,000 to 15,000 plus GST on this band, with any extra floors or long carries shown as their own line on the estimate.

Do you have an office in Gurugram?

No. In Gurugram the move is done by a verified partner team, and one coordinator from our head office at #705, Sector-7, Panchkula looks after the survey, the estimate, the schedule and your calls. The estimate and the GST invoice carry our name and GSTIN.

My society needs a lift booking and a deposit. Can you help?

Yes. Share the facility office rules with the coordinator once you have them. The partner team arrives inside the lift slot, pads the service lift and protects the lobby floor, which helps you get the deposit back. If the society allows moves only on weekdays, we plan the date around that first.

How much does it cost to shift from Gurugram to Noida?

Moves between Gurugram and Noida, Greater Noida or Ghaziabad are quoted only after a survey, because the price depends on the exact addresses and the route across Delhi. Call the 24-hour line, share both addresses and a rough list of goods, and you will get a written figure plus GST.

Can my employer pay for the move directly?

Yes. The invoice can be raised in your company's name with its GSTIN, and packing, transport and insurance are shown on separate lines. Many companies also want the estimate before approval, so we send it in writing after the survey.

Is it a problem to shift during the Gurugram monsoon?

It can be done safely with planning. We watch the forecast, avoid loading during a heavy spell, wrap mattresses and sofas in plastic and seal every carton with extra tape. If roads near your tower are known to flood, the truck waits on a dry stretch and goods are carried a little further.

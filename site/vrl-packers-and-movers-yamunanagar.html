Shifting in Yamunanagar: what we plan for

Yamunanagar and Jagadhri have grown into one urban area. Jagadhri is the older town, with crowded bazaars and houses packed close together, while Yamunanagar has planned HUDA sectors, older colonies near the railway and the camp area, and staff housing linked to the railway workshop and the thermal power plant. The Western Yamuna Canal runs through the district, and side roads near it can be narrow. Summers are hot, and the heavy monsoon can flood low-lying lanes for a few hours after a downpour.

Plywood and timber trade families

Yamunanagar is known across north India for its plywood and timber units, and Jagadhri has long been a centre for metal utensils. Business families in these trades often have large homes with heavy wooden furniture, and some want to move a small showroom or office with the house. The two are listed separately at the survey, and the office part is priced on our office rate card.

Staff and supervisors of the units move often, sometimes to another timber or plywood town and sometimes back home. For these smaller moves a 1 BHK within the up to 500 km band is roughly Rs 9,500 to 17,000 plus GST, and a 1 RK roughly Rs 8,500 to 15,000 plus GST.

Railway workshop and power plant transfers

The railway workshop at Jagadhri and the thermal power station near Yamunanagar bring regular transfers of employees who live in official quarters. These moves usually come with a handover date and an inspection of the quarter. The coordinator plans the packing so the house is empty a day before, which leaves time to clean and hand it over.

The invoice is an itemised GST bill from 1st VRL Packers and Movers. Employees of banks and PSUs who need a bill accepted for IBA claims can have the move arranged through our IBA-approved partner (code MUK-2068); mention this when you book.

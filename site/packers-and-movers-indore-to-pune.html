From Indore the truck heads south on NH52 by the Mhow side, comes down to the Narmada crossing at Khalghat and runs through Sendhwa and Shirpur to Dhule. From Dhule most drivers go by Malegaon to Nashik and then take the Nashik-Pune highway through Sinnar, Sangamner and Chakan, while some prefer the Ahmednagar side when that road is clearer. At about 630 km by road, the move uses the 500 to 1,500 km band of our chart, and household goods normally arrive in 2 to 3 days.

Most trucks take one night halt, around Dhule or Nashik. The Narmada ghat near Khalghat and the Sendhwa section are slow for heavy loads, and the stretch near Chakan on the Nashik-Pune highway is often jammed during the day. In the monsoon, the Nashik region gets heavy showers, so cartons are sealed with extra tape. Tolls on the way are added up and written as a separate line on the estimate.

Moving into Pune: what to plan for

Most Indore families settle on the western or eastern side of Pune: Hinjewadi, Wakad, Baner, Balewadi and Pimple Saudagar near the IT parks, or Kharadi, Viman Nagar, Wagholi and Hadapsar in the east. Societies there want a move-in form, a gate pass and a lift booking, much like the new towers on the Indore Super Corridor. Peak-hour traffic around Hinjewadi and Kharadi is heavy, so the verified partner team in Pune usually unloads mid-morning. The rain from June to September is steady, and the winter is mild and dry.

Common delivery areas are Hinjewadi, Wakad, Baner, Balewadi, Aundh, Pimple Saudagar, Pimpri, Chinchwad, Kothrud, Kharadi, Viman Nagar, Wagholi, Hadapsar, Magarpatta and Undri.

Our tip for this route: If you are joining a company in Hinjewadi or Chakan, ask HR for its preferred quotation format before the survey. The estimate can then be made in that format and approved in one round.

Why Indore families choose Pune

Pune draws from Indore in several ways. Software staff from Vijay Nagar offices and the Super Corridor campuses join product and services companies in Hinjewadi and Kharadi. Mechanical and production engineers from Pithampur move to auto plants around Chakan, Talegaon and Ranjangaon. Students who finish school or college in Indore go on to Pune universities, and parents sometimes follow a few years later when a child settles there.

Home shifting from Indore to Pune is often a move from an independent house or duplex to a tower flat. A family in Rajendra Nagar or Scheme No 140 used to its own staircase now has to fit lift timings and gate rules, so the survey checks both ends and plans the crew size for each.

Timing an Indore to Pune move

Pune schools start admissions early, so families with children tend to move in April or May, which are also the hottest months on the Dhule and Sendhwa stretch. In those weeks loading begins early in the morning and polished furniture gets extra wrap. Students more often move in June or July, when the rain on the Nashik side can slow the last part of the trip.

For a job move, fix the truck date once the offer letter is signed and the flat is chosen. Shared trucks fill up near month-end, so two weeks of notice is usually enough to get the day you want and the lift slots at both societies.

Common questions

How much do packers and movers charge from Indore to Pune?

For about 630 km, a 1 BHK costs Rs 15,000 - 27,000, a 2 BHK Rs 20,000 - 36,000 and a 3 BHK Rs 26,000 - 46,000, plus 18 per cent GST. The written estimate after the survey is the final price.

How long does shifting from Indore to Pune take?

Usually 2 to 3 days on a shared truck from the loading day. A dedicated truck is quicker but costs more. The driver's number is shared at loading, and the coordinator tells you if the arrival day changes.

Is there a low-cost option for students moving to Pune?

A student load of a bed, a study table and a few cartons is billed as a 1 RK, roughly Rs 13,000 to 23,000 plus GST on this route, with a scooter on its own line at roughly Rs 3,500 to 5,500 plus GST. If you can wait for a shared truck, the figure usually lands near the lower end.

What does bike shifting from Indore to Pune cost?

Roughly Rs 3,500 to 5,500 for a scooter such as a Jupiter, Rs 4,000 to 6,000 for a bike up to 150cc, Rs 5,500 to 8,000 for a 150cc to 350cc bike and Rs 8,000 to 11,500 for a premium bike above 350cc, plus GST.

Will my car arrive with the household goods in Pune?

Not always. The car goes on a separate carrier and may reach a day before or after the truck. A compact SUV is roughly Rs 12,000 to 19,000 plus GST on this route.

Do Pune societies allow shifting on Sundays?

Rules vary. Many allow moves only in fixed hours, some only on working days. Check with the society office first, and the unloading is planned in that window.

What if I reach Pune after the truck?

A friend or relative can receive the goods against the inventory sheet, or the goods can wait in a partner warehouse, charged separately and shown on the estimate.

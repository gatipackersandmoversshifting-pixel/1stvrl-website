Shifting in Dhuri: what we plan for

Dhuri is best known as a railway junction, where lines from Ludhiana, Patiala, Bathinda and Sangrur meet, and the station area is one of the busiest parts of town. The railway has brought generations of staff on transfer, and many older homes near the tracks belong to families who came here that way. A sugar mill on the edge of town draws cane trolleys from the surrounding villages through the winter, and the grain market fills during the wheat and paddy seasons. Housing is mostly independent houses, from older double-storey homes in the bazaar area to newer kothis along the Sangrur and Malerkotla roads. Dhuri is a subdivision of Sangrur district, which matters for pricing: towns of the same district within about 30 km are on our local rate.

Railway families on transfer

Station masters, loco pilots, track staff and their families have been moving in and out of Dhuri for decades. A transfer letter usually gives a fixed joining date, and the railway quarter has to be handed back clean. Tell the coordinator both dates at the first call, so packing finishes a day before the handover and the quarter can be inspected empty.

Railway moves often go to other junctions in Punjab or farther away. A 2 BHK from Dhuri to Kolkata, well over 1,500 km by road, is roughly Rs 27,000 to 47,000 plus GST. The GST invoice shows packing, labour and transport on separate lines and can be made in the employee's name for the transfer claim.

Cane season and the mill road

From around November to March, tractor trolleys stacked with sugarcane line the roads leading to the mill, sometimes for a long distance. A moving truck on those roads can get stuck behind them, so the partner team checks the route the day before and, where possible, uses another road into town or loads early.

The grain market adds its own rush in April for wheat and from October for paddy. If your house is near the mandi or the mill, mention it at the survey. The coordinator will suggest a time of day when the truck can park near your gate, and the estimate will show any extra carry needed.

Dhuri and Sangrur: one district, local rate

Many people work in Sangrur, the district headquarters, while living in Dhuri, and families shift between the two often. Since both are in Sangrur district and only about 18 km apart, these moves are priced on the local band. A 1 BHK is roughly Rs 5,000 to 9,000 plus GST and a 2 BHK roughly Rs 8,000 to 15,000 plus GST.

Government employees posted at the district offices often move when a new posting comes through. If the department is paying, the invoice can be raised in the officer's name. Bank and PSU staff who need IBA-format bills are served through our IBA-approved partner (code MUK-2068), and the coordinator explains the paperwork at the survey.

Malerkotla next door, but a different district

Malerkotla is only about 17 km from Dhuri, but since 2021 it has been a separate district, so a move there is on the up to 500 km band and not the local rate. A 1 BHK from Dhuri to Malerkotla is roughly Rs 9,500 to 17,000 plus GST. We explain this at the first call so there is no surprise on the estimate.

Dhuri shifting charges to Barnala and Ludhiana follow the same rule, as both towns are in other districts. A 2 BHK from Dhuri to Ludhiana, about 65 km by road, is roughly Rs 13,000 to 24,000 plus GST. Patiala, also in another district, is on the same band, and the exact figure depends on the items listed at the survey.

Packing for Malwa heat and winter fog

Summers in Dhuri are long and hot, with temperatures crossing 40 degrees in May and June. Wooden furniture and electronics are wrapped with padding and stretch film and loaded quickly, so nothing sits in the sun. Packing starts early in the morning, and the truck is covered before it leaves for long routes.

In December and January fog often settles over the fields and the roads to Sangrur and Ludhiana. On those days the driver leaves after visibility improves. For long routes like Delhi or Gurugram, the coordinator builds in a little extra time and tells you the realistic arrival day.

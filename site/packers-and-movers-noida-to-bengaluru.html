A truck from Noida does not need to cross Delhi to start this trip. It runs down the Yamuna Expressway from Greater Noida to Agra, picks up NH44 there and heads south through Gwalior, Jhansi and Sagar to Nagpur, then through Adilabad and Armoor into Hyderabad. South of Hyderabad the highway crosses the dry Rayalaseema country around Kurnool and Anantapur before the truck enters Bengaluru from the north side. At about 2,090 km by road, the move is charged on the 1,500 km plus band, the highest band of our chart, and a shared truck normally needs 7 to 9 days from the loading day.

Starting on the Yamuna Expressway spares the truck the worst of the NCR traffic. Over the next week the driver usually stops for the night two or three times, typically near Jhansi or Sagar, around Nagpur and close to Hyderabad or Kurnool. In winter the expressway has lower speed limits because of fog, and the Agra to Gwalior stretch is often misty at night, while central India is very hot in May. All tolls on the way are added up and written as one line on the estimate. The truck aims to reach north Bengaluru early in the morning, ahead of the Hebbal traffic.

Moving into Bengaluru: what to plan for

For someone used to a Noida AOA, a Bengaluru apartment association will feel familiar: it asks for a form, a copy of the lease or sale deed, an approved date and a lift booking. The surprise is usually the roads. Heavy vehicles are restricted on many city stretches during busy hours, and the Outer Ring Road near Bellandur and Marathahalli moves slowly for much of the day, so the partner team starts unloading soon after sunrise. Showers are common from June to October and can come at other times too, which is why plastic sheets for the lobby are part of the unloading kit.

The partner team most often unloads in Whitefield, Marathahalli, Bellandur, Sarjapur Road, HSR Layout, Koramangala, Electronic City, Hebbal, Thanisandra, Yelahanka, Hennur, JP Nagar, Bannerghatta Road and KR Puram.

Our tip for this route: Send the move-in request to your Bengaluru association the week the lease is signed. Approval can take a few working days, and it is better for the truck to leave Noida a day later than to stand outside a gate that has not cleared it.

What to sell, carry or send before a 2,090 km move

A long move is a good time to cut the load. Old coolers are of little use in Bengaluru's mild climate and can be sold in Noida, and heavy inverter batteries are often cheaper to replace than to ship. Cheap particle-board furniture rarely survives two rounds of dismantling, so it may be worth leaving behind. On the other hand, a good bed, a solid wood dining set and appliances in working order are usually worth sending, since buying them new in Bengaluru costs more than the freight.

Carry with you anything you will need in the first week, along with laptops, jewellery, certificates and medicines, because the truck arrives several days after your flight. Everything else is packed in layers suited to the trip: foam and cartons for electronics, corrugated sheet and stretch film for wooden pieces, and crates or boards for glass. The numbered inventory is signed in Noida and again in Bengaluru, so a missing carton is noticed at the door, not weeks later.

Common questions

How much do packers and movers charge from Noida to Bengaluru?

For about 2,090 km, a 1 BHK costs Rs 20,000 - 35,000, a 2 BHK Rs 27,000 - 47,000 and a 3 BHK Rs 34,000 - 60,000, plus 18 per cent GST. The written estimate after the survey is the final price.

How many days do goods take from Noida to Bengaluru?

Plan on 7 to 9 days from loading on a shared truck. A dedicated vehicle cuts this down but is priced higher. Winter fog on the Yamuna Expressway and around Gwalior sometimes adds a day, and the coordinator calls you if the date shifts.

What does car shifting from Noida to Bengaluru cost?

On a carrier, budget roughly Rs 13,000 to 22,000 for a hatchback such as a Swift, Rs 15,000 to 25,000 for a sedan like a City, Rs 17,000 to 28,000 for a Creta or Nexon class SUV and Rs 20,000 to 34,000 for an Innova or XUV700, plus GST. Photos of the car are taken at handover.

Can the invoice go to my new employer?

Yes. We raise the GST invoice in the company's name with its GSTIN and keep packing, freight and transit insurance on separate lines. If HR has a fixed quotation format, share it and the estimate is prepared in that format.

Can I drive my UP 16 car in Bengaluru?

Yes for a short stay. If it stays in Karnataka beyond a year, it has to be re-registered there, which needs an NOC from the Noida transport office. Hand over the registration certificate and insurance copy with the car.

Can the goods wait if my Bengaluru flat is delayed?

Yes. They can be kept in a partner warehouse, charged separately and shown on the estimate, and delivered when the flat is ready. Tell the coordinator as soon as you know about the delay.

Can two colleagues share one truck to Bengaluru?

Yes, if each household is surveyed and listed on its own inventory sheet. Cartons are colour-marked by family, and each gets its own estimate and GST invoice.

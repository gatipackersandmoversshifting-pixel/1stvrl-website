Shifting in Budhlada: what we plan for

Budhlada is a subdivision of Mansa district and sits close to the Haryana border, with Jakhal, Tohana and Ratia a short drive away. The railway line from Bathinda to Jakhal passes through the town, giving direct trains towards Delhi, and the bus stand links it to Mansa, Patiala and the Haryana side. A large grain market handles wheat and paddy, and cotton from the surrounding villages has long been the area's main cash crop. Smaller towns such as Boha and Bareta lie within the same subdivision. Most families live in independent houses on long plots, and newer homes have come up along the Mansa and Boha roads. Many households also keep a link with their village fields, so moves often include farm items alongside the household goods.

Budhlada families often have relatives, land or jobs across the border in Haryana, and moves to Jakhal, Tohana or Ratia are common. Mansa district postings and local moves

Teachers, police staff and officials posted in Mansa district often move between Budhlada, Mansa town, Boha and Bareta when a transfer comes through. Mansa town is the headquarters of the same district, so a move between Budhlada and Mansa is on the local band: a 1 BHK is roughly Rs 5,000 to 9,000, a 2 BHK roughly Rs 8,000 to 15,000 and a 3 BHK roughly Rs 12,000 to 21,000 plus GST.

If the department is reimbursing the move, the GST invoice can be made in the employee's name with packing, labour and transport on separate lines. Cotton picking and the market rush

From September to November, cotton picking and the paddy harvest keep the roads to the Budhlada market full of trolleys. A truck loading outside a house on those roads can block traffic, so the partner team chooses an early start or an afternoon slot after the market quietens. The plan is set the day before.

Farming families often want to finish a move before the harvest or wait until the crop is sold. If you are flexible, tell the coordinator the weeks that suit you, and we will suggest days when labour is easier to arrange and the roads towards Mansa and Patiala are lighter.

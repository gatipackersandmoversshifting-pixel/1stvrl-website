From Jaipur the truck heads north-east on NH48 past Shahpura, Kotputli, Behror and Neemrana, crosses into Haryana near Bawal, passes Dharuhera, runs through Manesar and Gurugram and enters Delhi near the airport side of the city. The road distance is about 280 km, so this move is priced on the up to 500 km band of our rate card. A shared truck usually delivers in 1 to 2 days; a dedicated vehicle can unload the same evening or the next morning if loading in Jaipur finishes early.

There is normally no night halt. The truck leaves Jaipur early, takes the Delhi Road out past Amer, and runs up the six-lane highway past Shahpura and Kotputli. Traffic thickens near Behror, Neemrana and the Haryana border, and the slowest part of the whole trip is usually the run through Manesar and Gurugram. Toll plazas on NH48 are added together and shown as one line on the estimate. In December and January, morning fog in Haryana can delay the arrival by a few hours.

Moving into Delhi: what to plan for

Delivering into Delhi means working around the daytime limits on goods vehicles on many city roads, so most unloading is planned for early morning or late evening. DDA flats and builder floors often have no lift and narrow stairs, which sets the crew size. In Gurugram the picture is different: tall societies with service lifts, a security register at the gate and a fixed slot for moving in. Both ends get hot in May and June, so the load is not left standing in the sun.

Common delivery areas are Gurugram (DLF Phases, Golf Course Road, Sohna Road, Dwarka Expressway sectors), Dwarka, Vasant Kunj, Janakpuri, Rajouri Garden, Saket, Malviya Nagar, Rohini, Pitampura, Mayur Vihar, Noida and Faridabad.

Our tip for this route: If your new home is in Gurugram, ask the society for the move-in slot as soon as the date is fixed. A truck that leaves Jaipur at dawn can be at the gate by early afternoon.

Who moves from Jaipur to Delhi and Gurugram

Most people on this route are moving for work. A job offer in Cyber City or on Golf Course Road, a posting at a central ministry, or a promotion that comes with a transfer to the NCR office are the usual reasons. Students heading to Delhi colleges and families joining children who already work in the capital make up much of the rest. Because the distance is short, many customers expect a simple job, but the two ends are very different: an independent house in Mansarovar or a flat in a Jagatpura tower at one end, and a lift slot in Gurugram or a third-floor walk-up in Delhi at the other.

The partner team in Jaipur packs and loads, and a partner team in the NCR unloads and places furniture, both following one inventory and one plan from our Panchkula coordinator. If you are still deciding between a house in Delhi and one in Gurugram, the Jaipur to Delhi shifting charges in the charts above apply to both, so you can fix the move date before the final address and update it before loading.

Jaipur to Delhi shifting charges and packing

On the up to 500 km band, a 1 RK costs Rs 8,500 to 15,000, a 2 BHK Rs 13,000 to 24,000, a 3 BHK Rs 17,000 to 32,000 and a 4 BHK or villa Rs 23,000 to 41,000, plus GST. A small office of up to 10 workstations moving from Jaipur to Gurugram or Delhi costs Rs 25,000 to 45,000 and a medium office of 10 to 30 workstations Rs 50,000 to 1,00,000, plus GST. These figures include packing, loading, transport and unloading with up to one flight of stairs or a working lift.

A short trip does not mean lighter packing. The highway is good, but the city ends are full of speed breakers, potholed service lanes and tight turns into society gates, and that is where loose items break. Glass tops get corner guards, the fridge travels upright, the washing machine drum is secured, and wooden beds are opened and their bolts bagged and taped to the frame. In summer, polished furniture is wrapped in paper before sheet so the polish is not marked by the heat inside the truck.

Common questions

How much do packers and movers charge from Jaipur to Delhi?

For about 280 km, a 1 BHK costs Rs 9,500 - 17,000, a 2 BHK Rs 13,000 - 24,000 and a 3 BHK Rs 17,000 - 32,000, plus 18 per cent GST. The written estimate after the survey is the final price.

How long does Jaipur to Delhi shifting take?

On a shared truck it is usually 1 to 2 days. With a dedicated vehicle, a 1 or 2 BHK packed in the morning in Jaipur can often be unloaded the same evening or the next morning, depending on the entry hours at the Delhi or Gurugram address.

What does car transport from Jaipur to Delhi cost?

On a car carrier a hatchback costs Rs 5,500 to 9,000, a sedan Rs 6,500 to 10,500 and a compact SUV such as a Creta or Nexon Rs 7,500 to 12,000, plus GST. The car is photographed at pickup and listed on the inventory.

Is the price the same for Gurugram, Noida and Delhi?

All three are in the up to 500 km band from Jaipur, so the same chart applies. The final written figure after the survey can differ slightly with the floor, the lift and how far the truck can park from your door.

When can the truck unload in Delhi?

Goods vehicles are limited on many Delhi roads in the daytime, so unloading in Delhi is usually early morning or late evening. Gurugram societies set their own slots. The coordinator fixes the time with you once the truck has left Jaipur.

Can you shift an office from Jaipur to Gurugram over a weekend?

Yes. Packing starts on Friday evening, the truck runs on Saturday and desks are set up on Sunday. A small office of up to 10 workstations costs Rs 25,000 to 45,000 plus GST on this route, with desk setup at Rs 800 to 2,000 each.

Do I need to be at both ends on the same day?

Not necessarily. If the truck unloads the next day, you can travel by train or road after loading in Jaipur and still be at the Delhi or Gurugram address in time. If you cannot be there, name a person to check the inventory.

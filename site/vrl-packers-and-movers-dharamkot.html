Shifting in Dharamkot: what we plan for

This Dharamkot should not be confused with the hill village of the same name above McLeod Ganj in Himachal; the Punjab town is a municipal council and the headquarters of Dharamkot tehsil in Moga district. Its population was about 19,000 in the 2011 census and has grown since, as families from the villages build in town for schools and clinics. The tehsil is wide and rural, with villages such as Kot Mohammad Khan, Kaila, Kahnewala, Lohgarh, Ramgarh and Khera Daroli spread across wheat and paddy fields, and Kot Ise Khan is the other town of the tehsil. Most traffic leaves Dharamkot on the road south to Moga, from where trucks join the highways towards Ludhiana, Ferozepur or Bathinda. Housing is almost all independent: older homes in the bazaar, new kothis on the Moga road and large farmhouses with store rooms in the villages. Summers are hot and humid, and December fog over the fields can be heavy.

Punjab's Dharamkot, not the Himachal one

Searches for Dharamkot often mix up our Moga district town with the village above Dharamshala. Packers and movers Dharamkot for village kothis

A large share of Dharamkot jobs start in a village farmhouse rather than in town. These houses tend to have big rooms, double-door almirahs, heavy sheesham beds and store rooms full of trunks. The partner team checks doors and stairs first, opens furniture that comes apart and bags every screw with its panel.

The approach road matters too. Where a village lane is too narrow or soft for a loaded truck, the truck waits at the nearest firm stretch of road and goods are carried or trolleyed to it. That arrangement is noted at the survey and priced into the estimate, so the day goes to plan.

Home shifting in Dharamkot through the seasons

For home shifting in Dharamkot in May and June, packing starts at first light and the heaviest carrying is done before noon. Electronics travel in padded cartons at the front of the truck, away from the hot rear doors.

In the monsoon, cartons are taped along every seam and mattresses are sealed in corrugated sheet and film. From mid-December, fog can sit over the Moga road until late morning, so longer trips leave once it lifts, and wheat and paddy harvest weeks are planned around the trolley traffic near the mandi.

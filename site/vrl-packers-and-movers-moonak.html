Shifting in Moonak: what we plan for

Moonak was once called Akalgarh, and its old fort, the Qila Mubarik, still stands at the heart of the town. It is a subdivision of Sangrur district, close to the Haryana border, with Khanauri a short drive away and Jakhal and Tohana across the state line. The Ghaggar curves around the area, and floods have hit Moonak and its villages several times, including in 2019 and 2023. Farming is the main work, but the town also has small industries: agricultural machinery workshops, PVC pipe units, rice mills, brick kilns and furniture makers. A Punjabi University college and other colleges serve students from the surrounding villages. Homes are mostly independent houses, with older ones in the lanes near the fort and the bazaar and newer ones along the Khanauri, Lehragaga and Patran roads. Summer temperatures can cross 44 degrees in June.

Living with the Ghaggar

Families in Moonak know the river well. In a heavy monsoon the Ghaggar can break its banks, and some villages and low-lying colonies have been cut off for days. If your move falls between July and September, tell the coordinator where the house is, and the partner team will check the road the evening before and keep a spare day in the plan.

Some families choose to shift goods to a relative's house on higher ground before the rains, then move again later. A short local shift like this inside Moonak is on the local band, roughly Rs 5,000 to 9,000 plus GST for a 1 BHK. Furniture is wrapped in plastic under the blankets, and cartons are stacked off the floor.

Workshops, mills and small factories

Moonak's machinery workshops, pipe units and furniture makers sometimes need their office, stock or light equipment moved when they expand or relocate. The survey separates the household goods from the business items, so you can see the cost of each, and the GST invoice can be raised in the firm's name.

A small office of up to 10 workstations moved within Moonak or to Khanauri is roughly Rs 12,000 to 25,000 plus GST on our office rate card, and a server rack is roughly Rs 3,000 to 8,000 plus GST extra. Heavy machines that need a crane or a specialist rigging team are not part of a household move, and the coordinator tells you this plainly at the survey.

Lanes near the Qila Mubarik

The streets around the old fort and the bazaar are narrow, and many houses there share walls and have steep stairs. The truck usually waits on the nearest open road while goods come out by hand or on a trolley. The survey counts the distance and steps, so the extra carry is on the estimate from the start.

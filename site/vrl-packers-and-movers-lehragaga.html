Shifting in Lehragaga: what we plan for

Lehragaga is a subdivision headquarters in the south-east of Sangrur district, close to the Haryana towns of Jakhal and Tohana. The railway line from Jakhal towards Dhuri and Ludhiana passes through the town, so trains link it both to the Malwa and to the Delhi side. The grain market is the centre of the local economy, with wheat in April and paddy from October, and rice shellers and commission agents' shops line the roads out of town. The Baba Hira Singh Bhattal Institute of Engineering and Technology brings students and teaching staff to Lehragaga. Most homes are independent houses, older ones close to the bazaar and the station, newer kothis on the Sunam and Jakhal roads. The Ghaggar flows not far to the south-east, and in heavy monsoon years its floods have reached villages of the subdivision, most recently in 2023. Summers are hot and dry, and winters are foggy.

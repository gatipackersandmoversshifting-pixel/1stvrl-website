Kolkata to Hyderabad at a glance

1,490 kmRoad distance, approx.
5 to 7 daysHousehold transit time
NH16 and NH65Main route
500 to 1,500 kmPrice band used below

From Kolkata the truck takes NH16 to Kharagpur and then down the coast through Balasore, Cuttack, Bhubaneswar, Berhampur, Visakhapatnam and Rajahmundry to Vijayawada. There it turns inland onto NH65, which runs north-west through Suryapet to Hyderabad. The road distance is about 1,490 km, so this route falls just inside the 500 to 1,500 km price band rather than the 1,500 km plus band, which keeps it cheaper than the other long routes from Kolkata. On a shared truck, household goods usually reach in 5 to 7 days.

Most trucks spend three nights on the road, typically near Bhubaneswar, near Srikakulam, and near Eluru or Vijayawada, before the last day inland. The NH65 stretch from Vijayawada past Suryapet is a straight four-lane road through farmland and quick to drive. The coast before it is the slower part, with long bridges, town bypasses and heavy freight traffic near the ports. Tolls on both highways are added into one line on the estimate, and the truck enters Hyderabad from the LB Nagar side before heading to your address.

Moving into Hyderabad: what to plan for

Most moves into Hyderabad finish in a gated community or a large apartment complex in Gachibowli, Kondapur, Kokapet, Manikonda or Kukatpally. The association usually asks for a move-in request, a lift booking and the vehicle number, and some allow trucks only between set hours. Parts of Secunderabad and the older city have independent houses on narrower roads, where a smaller vehicle may be needed. Hyderabad is dry and hot from March to May, very different from Kolkata's humidity, and the monsoon months bring short, heavy showers.

Common delivery areas are Gachibowli, Kondapur, Madhapur, HITEC City, Financial District, Kokapet, Narsingi, Manikonda, Kukatpally, Miyapur, Nizampet, Secunderabad, Uppal and LB Nagar.

Our tip for this route: If you are moving into a new tower in Kokapet or the Financial District, ask the builder whether the service lift is already working. In newly handed-over buildings it sometimes is not, and that changes the crew size and the plan for heavy items.

Kolkata to Hyderabad packers and movers: who moves and why

Hyderabad's IT corridor and its pharma industry draw many engineers and scientists from Kolkata, and a good number of those moves are paid for by the employer. Kolkata to Hyderabad packers and movers also handle central government, bank and defence transfers, since Hyderabad and Secunderabad have large establishments of all three. A typical booking is a 2 BHK family move from south Kolkata or Salt Lake into a gated community near the Outer Ring Road, often with a car and a scooter on the same estimate.

The trip runs along the coast for most of its length, so packing is set for humidity first. Wooden furniture is wrapped in foam and stretch film, steel almirahs in corrugated sheet, and the fridge, TV and washing machine go in padded cartons or their original boxes. Hyderabad's drier air is kinder to wood, but a quick change from damp to dry can open the joints of older furniture, so we suggest unwrapping polished pieces indoors and away from the strong afternoon sun.

Common questions

How much do packers and movers charge from Kolkata to Hyderabad?

For about 1,490 km, a 1 BHK costs Rs 15,000 - 27,000, a 2 BHK Rs 20,000 - 36,000 and a 3 BHK Rs 26,000 - 46,000, plus 18 per cent GST. The written estimate after the survey is the final price.

How long does Kolkata to Hyderabad shifting take?

Usually 5 to 7 days on a shared truck. The coastal part of the trip can see heavy rain or a storm warning between June and November, which may add a day. The coordinator keeps you posted from each halt.

Why is Kolkata to Hyderabad cheaper than Kolkata to Bengaluru or Chennai?

Because the road distance, about 1,490 km, sits inside the 500 to 1,500 km band. Bengaluru and Chennai are more than 1,500 km away, so they use the higher band. A 2 BHK, for example, costs Rs 20,000 to 36,000 plus GST to Hyderabad, against Rs 27,000 to 47,000 plus GST to Bengaluru.

Can you ship a luxury car from Kolkata to Hyderabad?

Yes. A BMW, Audi or Mercedes travels in an enclosed carrier for Rs 28,000 to 45,000 plus GST on this route. The car is photographed from all sides at pickup in Kolkata, the odometer reading is noted, and the same check is done at delivery in Hyderabad.

Can you deliver to Uppal or LB Nagar on the east side of Hyderabad?

Yes. These areas are on the side where the truck enters the city from Vijayawada, so delivery there is often a little quicker. Independent houses in these areas can sit on narrower roads, so share the lane width and floor during the survey.

Do you pack puja items and idols separately?

Yes. Idols, brass items, conch shells and the puja set are wrapped one by one in soft paper and bubble sheet, packed in a small marked carton and loaded where nothing heavy can press on them. Tell the partner team at the survey which items you want packed this way.

Can I send a scooter and a car together from Kolkata to Hyderabad?

Yes, on the same estimate. A hatchback on a carrier costs Rs 9,000 to 15,000 and a sedan Rs 10,500 to 17,000, plus GST, and a scooter costs Rs 3,500 to 5,500 plus GST on this route. The scooter travels with the household goods and the car on a separate carrier.

The truck runs south from Vadodara on NH48 through Bharuch, Surat, Valsad and Vapi, crosses into Maharashtra at Achhad and continues through Palghar district towards Bhiwandi. Instead of entering Mumbai, it skirts the city on the Bhiwandi and Panvel side and climbs onto the Mumbai-Pune Expressway, which takes it up the Bhor ghat past Khalapur, Khandala and Lonavala to Talegaon and into Pune. The road distance is about 560 km, so this page uses the 500 to 1,500 km band of our chart, and household goods usually arrive in 2 to 3 days.

Drivers often make one overnight stop near the Maharashtra border or in Palghar district, then cross the Bhiwandi and Panvel stretch early to avoid the worst freight traffic around Mumbai. The expressway climb to Lonavala is slow for loaded trucks, and in the monsoon fog on the ghat cuts speed further. The truck usually enters Pune in the morning. Tolls on NH48 and the expressway are combined into one line on the estimate.

Traffic around the IT parks is heavy at office hours, so the partner team in Pune usually unloads in the late morning or the evening. How the truck is packed for the Bhor ghat

The climb from the Konkan plain to Lonavala on the expressway is steep and long, so the loading order is planned for it. Heavy items such as the fridge, washing machine and almirahs go in first against the front wall, mattresses and sofas act as cushions between layers, and cartons are stacked in tight rows so nothing shifts on the gradient. Table legs, bed corners and glass tops get corrugated sheet and bubble wrap.

In the monsoon the ghat section is often in fog and rain, and Pune itself can have showers on most days from June to September. Ask for sofas and mattresses in plastic covers and keep a small essentials box with you, so the first night in Pune does not depend on finding the right carton.

Common questions

How much do packers and movers charge from Vadodara to Pune?

For about 560 km, a 1 BHK costs Rs 15,000 - 27,000, a 2 BHK Rs 20,000 - 36,000 and a 3 BHK Rs 26,000 - 46,000, plus 18 per cent GST. The written estimate after the survey is the final price.

How many days do goods take from Vadodara to Pune?

Usually 2 to 3 days from loading on a shared truck. A dedicated truck loaded in Vadodara in the morning can often unload in Pune the next day, at a higher price. The driver's number is shared with you on loading day.

Does the truck go through Mumbai city on the way to Pune?

No. It stays on the highway around the edge of Mumbai on the Bhiwandi and Panvel side and then takes the expressway to Pune, which keeps a loaded truck out of city traffic and entry limits.

What does bike shifting from Vadodara to Pune cost?

About Rs 3,500 to 5,500 for a scooter such as an Activa, Rs 4,000 to 6,000 for a bike up to 150cc such as a Pulsar 150, Rs 5,500 to 8,000 for a 150cc to 350cc bike such as a Classic 350, and Rs 8,000 to 11,500 for a premium bike such as a Himalayan 450, plus GST.

Can someone else receive the goods in Pune if my flight is later?

Yes. Anyone you name can receive the goods and check them against the signed inventory. Share their name and number with the coordinator before the truck leaves Vadodara.

Do you deliver to Pimpri-Chinchwad and Talegaon too?

Yes. Pimpri, Chinchwad, Akurdi, Talegaon and Chakan are on the same 500 to 1,500 km chart as Pune city, and Talegaon lies just off the expressway before the city.

Is Vadodara to Pune cheaper than Vadodara to Mumbai?

No. Mumbai is about 415 km and falls in the up to 500 km band, while Pune at about 560 km is in the 500 to 1,500 km band, so a 2 BHK to Pune is about Rs 20,000 to 36,000 plus GST against Rs 13,000 to 24,000 to Mumbai.

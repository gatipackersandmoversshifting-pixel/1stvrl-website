Bengaluru to Hyderabad at a glance

570 kmRoad distance, approx.
2 to 3 daysHousehold transit time
NH44Main route
500 to 1,500 kmPrice band used below

The truck leaves Bengaluru past Hebbal and the airport at Devanahalli and runs north on NH44, a fast divided highway for most of the trip. It crosses into Andhra Pradesh after Bagepalli, passes Penukonda and Anantapur, reaches Kurnool on the Tungabhadra, then enters Telangana and runs through Jadcherla to Shamshabad on Hyderabad's southern edge, where the Outer Ring Road leads to Gachibowli and the western suburbs. At about 570 km this route sits just inside the 500 to 1,500 km price band, so it costs more than a sub-500 km move like Chennai but much less than a run to the north. On a shared truck household goods usually reach in 2 to 3 days, and a dedicated vehicle can unload the next day.

This is usually a one-night trip. The truck aims to clear Bengaluru's northern exits before the morning traffic on Bellary Road, crosses the Andhra border by mid-morning and halts overnight near Kurnool or Anantapur, depending on loading time. NH44 here runs through open, dry country with long straight sections and several toll plazas, and the full toll is shown as one line in the estimate. The Kurnool to Hyderabad stretch is smooth and fast, so most trucks reach Shamshabad early on the second day and wait there for the community's slot rather than queue at your gate. In the monsoon the load is covered with a tarpaulin, though this belt gets far less rain than the coastal routes.

Moving into Hyderabad: what to plan for

Most Bengaluru to Hyderabad moves end in gated communities in Gachibowli, Kondapur, Nanakramguda, Kokapet, Narsingi or along the Outer Ring Road, with a growing share in Kukatpally, Miyapur, Bachupally and Kompally to the north. Associations usually want a move-in request, the truck number and crew names a day ahead, and allow unloading only in a fixed window. Many high-rises have a separate service lift, so the move is quick once the truck is inside the gate. Hyderabad is much hotter than Bengaluru from March to June, with afternoons above 40 degrees, so unloading is planned for the morning or evening and heat-sensitive items are kept out of the midday sun. The July to September rains can flood underpasses, so the route inside the city is chosen on the day.

Common delivery areas are Gachibowli, Kondapur, HITEC City, Madhapur, Nanakramguda, Financial District, Kokapet, Narsingi, Manikonda, Kukatpally, Miyapur, Bachupally, Kompally and Secunderabad.

Our tip for this route: Hyderabad associations often take two to three days to approve a move-in. Apply as soon as you sign the new lease, and send us the approval so the truck is booked against a confirmed slot.

Common questions

How much do packers and movers charge from Bengaluru to Hyderabad?

For about 570 km, a 1 BHK costs Rs 15,000 - 27,000, a 2 BHK Rs 20,000 - 36,000 and a 3 BHK Rs 26,000 - 46,000, plus 18 per cent GST. The written estimate after the survey is the final price.

How long does Bengaluru to Hyderabad shifting take?

Usually 2 to 3 days on a shared truck. A dedicated vehicle can load in Bengaluru in the morning and unload in Hyderabad the next morning, subject to your community's move-in window.

What does car transport from Bengaluru to Hyderabad cost?

A hatchback costs Rs 9,000 to 15,000, a sedan like a City or Verna Rs 10,500 to 17,000 and a compact SUV Rs 12,000 to 19,000 on this route, plus GST.

Can you shift a single room or 1 RK from Bengaluru to Hyderabad?

Yes. A 1 RK or studio costs Rs 13,000 to 23,000 plus GST on this route. For just a few items, a shared truck keeps the cost at the lower end of the band.

Can you move my bike from Bengaluru to Hyderabad?

Yes. A scooter costs Rs 3,500 to 5,500, a commuter bike up to 150cc Rs 4,000 to 6,000 and a 150cc to 350cc bike Rs 5,500 to 8,000 on this route, plus GST.

Can you shift an IT office from Bengaluru to Hyderabad?

Yes. A small office of up to 10 workstations costs Rs 40,000 to 70,000, and a medium office of 10 to 30 workstations Rs 80,000 to 1,50,000 on this route, plus GST. A server rack is Rs 3,000 to 8,000 extra.

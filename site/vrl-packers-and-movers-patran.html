Shifting in Patran: what we plan for

Patran grew from a small village of Patiala state into one of the bigger rice markets of the region, with well over a hundred rice shellers around the town and a grain market that works at full stretch from October. It is also known for its used-car bazaar, where dozens of dealers line the main roads. Devotees come to the Shri Khatu Shyam temple, known as Patran Dham, and to the Mai Dera shrine with its old trees and Shiva temple. Maharaja Bhupindra Singh of Patiala had the Bhupindra Sagar lake made near Patran for hunting. The town sits on roads to Samana, Sangrur, Kaithal and Tohana, and the Delhi to Amritsar to Katra Expressway has a toll plaza at Ghagga nearby. The Ghaggar flows close to the south, and the area flooded in 2023. Homes are mostly independent houses, with newer kothis along the Samana and Sangrur roads.

Rice shellers and the paddy rush

From October to December, trolleys of paddy queue on every road into Patran, and the shellers run day and night. A household truck stuck in that line can lose hours, so the partner team loads early in the morning or late in the afternoon, after the day's arrivals at the mandi slow down. Tell the coordinator if your house is on a sheller road.

Sheller owners, managers and accountants also move house, often with office furniture, files and a safe from the business. Festival days at Patran Dham

The Khatu Shyam temple draws large crowds on its main festival days, and the roads near the temple fill with cars, buses and stalls. If your home is close by, the coordinator checks the date against the temple calendar and suggests a quieter day, or plans the loading for early morning before the devotees arrive.

The Mai Dera shrine has its own gatherings too. These timing changes never change the rate band. The plan is simply noted on the estimate, and the partner team arrives at the agreed hour with the packing material and the truck.

Flood weeks and the roads to Delhi

The Ghaggar flood of 2023 showed how quickly fields and link roads around Patran can go under water. For moves in the monsoon, the route is checked the evening before, furniture is wrapped in plastic under the blankets, and the coordinator keeps a spare day in the plan in case a road closes.

For Delhi and Gurugram, trucks usually run through Haryana. Where sections of the new expressway are open and faster, the driver may use them. In December and January fog can hang over the plains until late morning, so long-distance trucks leave after the road clears, and you get a realistic arrival time.

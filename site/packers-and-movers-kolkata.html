Shifting in Kolkata: what we plan for

Home shifting in Kolkata depends a great deal on the age of the building. In north Kolkata, around Shyambazar, Baghbazar and Shobhabazar, many families live in old two- and three-storey houses on lanes so narrow that a full-size truck cannot turn in, so goods are carried to the main road or brought out in a smaller vehicle first. These walk-ups rarely have a lift, the staircases are steep, and heavy teak almirahs often have to be dismantled. Many city roads have daytime no-entry hours for goods vehicles, so loading is usually fixed for early morning or late evening. From June to September the monsoon can leave low-lying streets waterlogged after a heavy spell, during Durga Puja pandals and crowds close lanes for days, and in April and May sudden nor'wester storms can arrive in the afternoon.

In south Kolkata, apartment blocks in Ballygunge, Alipore, Jodhpur Park and Lake Gardens often have a small lift and a caretaker who wants advance notice, so the coordinator calls the building a day before. Older houses in Bhowanipore and Kalighat get a larger crew for the stairs. East of the EM Bypass, the towers of New Town and Rajarhat usually have a service lift, a move-in slot and a security desk that asks for a gate pass, which we arrange with the management office. In Salt Lake, block-wise houses normally have space in front for the truck, which makes loading quicker. Every booking starts with a survey, by video call or in person, where the partner team notes the floor, lift size, lane width and parking.

Home shifting in Kolkata: an area-by-area guide

Most homes in south Kolkata, from Tollygunge and Jadavpur to Behala and Garia, are apartment buildings of four to eight floors on reasonably wide roads, so a mid-size truck can usually park close by. Central areas such as Park Street, Elliot Road and Bhowanipore mix old mansion flats with newer blocks, and daytime parking there is tight. For a move inside the Kolkata Municipal Corporation area, for example Kasba to Behala or Gariahat to Alipore, the local band of the rate chart applies. The survey records the floor, lift size and parking at each address, so the crew size is right on the day.

North Kolkata needs the most planning. The old neighbourhoods around Shyambazar, Hatibagan and College Street have narrow lanes, shared courtyards and wooden or stone staircases, and the crew often carries goods some distance to the truck. For packers and movers in Salt Lake and New Town the work is different: wide sector roads, planned blocks and high-rise towers with service lifts. A move inside Salt Lake, New Town or Rajarhat is charged on the local band. Salt Lake, New Town, Rajarhat, Dum Dum, Baranagar and Lake Town are in North 24 Parganas, and Howrah is a separate district, so for a move between Kolkata city and any of them the coordinator gives a written figure after the survey.

Monsoon, Durga Puja and the right time to move in Kolkata

The south-west monsoon usually reaches Kolkata in June and stays until about September. A heavy downpour can leave low-lying streets under water for hours, and furniture cannot be loaded safely through deep water. In these months the partner team wraps cartons in plastic sheet, keeps mattresses in sealed covers and carries tarpaulins for the walk from the door to the vehicle. If a heavy spell is forecast, the coordinator may shift the loading by a few hours rather than risk soaked furniture. Humidity stays high for most of the year, so wooden pieces are wrapped before they leave the room.

Durga Puja falls in September or October, and for the week around it large parts of the city are taken over by pandals, lights and crowds. Lanes in both north and south Kolkata can be closed to vehicles, and police diversions change from day to day. Moves still happen, but if you can, finish a week before Mahalaya or wait until after Lakshmi Puja. Winter, from November to February, is the most comfortable season for shifting, with dry weather and mild days. In April and May, afternoon nor'westers bring sudden wind and rain, so loading starts early in the morning.

Yes, all three are covered. A move inside Salt Lake, New Town or Rajarhat is charged on the local band of the house chart. For a move between Kolkata city and Salt Lake, New Town, Dum Dum or Howrah, the coordinator gives you a written figure after the survey.

My house in north Kolkata is on a narrow lane with no lift. Can you still shift it?

Yes. The survey checks the lane width, the staircase and the nearest point where a truck can stand. Goods are carried to that point or brought out in a smaller vehicle, heavy almirahs are dismantled where needed, and any long stair carry is shown as a separate line on the estimate.

Can I shift house during Durga Puja?

You can, but expect closed lanes, police diversions and slower loading in the days around Saptami to Dashami. If your dates are flexible, move a week before Mahalaya or after Lakshmi Puja. If you must move during the festival, tell us at the survey so loading is planned for the quietest hours.

Yes. Cars go on a car carrier, and bikes are padded and loaded with the household goods. On 1,500 km plus routes such as Kolkata to Delhi, a hatchback costs Rs 13,000 to 22,000 and a scooter Rs 4,500 to 7,000, plus GST. There is no local car or bike rate within the city.

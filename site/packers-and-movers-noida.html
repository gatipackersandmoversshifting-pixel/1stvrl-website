Shifting in Noida: what we plan for

Home shifting in Noida follows the city's sector plan more than its roads. The older plotted sectors in the teens, twenties and thirties, such as Sector 15, 19, 26 and 31, are mostly independent houses and builder-style floors with an outside staircase and no lift, while the group housing sectors from the 70s onwards, along the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway and in Sector 137, 143 and 150, are high-rise towers run by an Apartment Owners Association. Almost every AOA wants a move-out or move-in form, a no-dues slip from the maintenance office and a booked service lift, and many fix the hours when a truck may enter the campus. Urban villages such as Mamura, Baraula, Bhangel and Sarfabad have rented rooms on lanes too tight for a full truck. In July and August a heavy spell of rain can fill underpasses and low service roads quickly, so loading days in the monsoon are fixed with the forecast open.

The survey starts with two questions: which tower and floor, and what the AOA allows. For towers in Sector 76, 78, 100, 128 and along the Expressway, the partner team books the service lift through the maintenance desk, lays floor sheets in the lobby and pads the lift walls. In plotted sectors such as Sector 27, 36, 41 and 50, beds and wardrobes are often opened up on the upper floor and brought down the outside stairs by hand. In the urban villages a small tempo carries cartons to a truck parked on the sector road. Greater Noida West, where several societies hand over flats in the same months, needs the slot to be booked early, because service lifts there fill up on weekends.

Home shifting in Noida: sector by sector

Packers and movers in Sector 15, Sector 19 and the other early sectors near the DND Flyway mostly deal with two- and three-storey houses, where the furniture is heavier and older and the outside staircase is the slow part of the day. Packers and movers in Sector 18 and Sector 38 work around the market and mall traffic, so loading there is set for the early morning before shops open. Further south, the towers in Sector 93, 100 and 104 near the Expressway have good service lifts but long walks from the tower to the main gate, and trolleys are sent so cartons do not have to be carried the whole way.

The Expressway sectors from 128 to 168 are newer, with tall towers, wide internal roads and strict AOA timings, and many residents work in the corporate offices nearby. Noida Extension, also called Greater Noida West, has some of the largest housing societies in the region, around Gaur Chowk, where move days cluster at the start and end of the month. Families in Greater Noida, around Pari Chowk, Alpha, Beta and Knowledge Park, often live in plotted houses or lower towers, and students from the colleges along the Yamuna Expressway usually move a single room with a bike.

Noida shifting charges for local and nearby moves

A move inside Noida, or between Noida and Delhi, Greater Noida, Greater Noida West or Faridabad, is priced on the local band of our house chart, because these are treated as one metro area. On that band a 1 RK costs about Rs 3,000 to 6,500, a 1 BHK about Rs 5,000 to 9,000, a 2 BHK about Rs 8,000 to 15,000 and a 3 BHK about Rs 12,000 to 21,000, plus GST. A 4 BHK or a large independent house is about Rs 17,000 to 29,000 plus GST. So a 2 BHK going from Sector 50 to Greater Noida West, or from Sector 137 to Mayur Vihar, sits on the same local figures.

Moves between Noida and Gurugram or Ghaziabad are not shown on the local band; they get a written figure after the survey, because the cost depends on the exact addresses and the route the truck takes. Mumbai falls in the 500 to 1,500 km band, and Bengaluru and Chennai in the 1,500 km plus band. Pune, Hyderabad and Kolkata are all close to the 1,500 km line by road, so for those cities the band is fixed in writing after the survey. Office shifting and staff transfers in Noida

Noida has a large office base, from IT and back-office companies in Sector 62, 63 and 125 to 144, to media houses in Film City and many small firms in the office blocks of the older sectors such as Sector 2, 3 and 16. Office shifting in Noida is planned around working hours: a small office of up to 10 workstations moving within the city costs about Rs 12,000 to 25,000 plus GST, and a medium office of 10 to 30 workstations about Rs 25,000 to 60,000 plus GST. Season, air quality and paperwork for a Noida move

Each season changes the plan. May and June are very hot, so the crew starts packing early and keeps polished furniture out of direct sun while it waits on the road. In the monsoon, cartons get extra tape, mattresses go into plastic and the truck is parked away from roads that usually flood. From November to January, smog and fog are common across the NCR. When the higher stages of the Graded Response Action Plan are in force, many older diesel goods vehicles are kept out of Delhi, so for a Noida to Delhi move the coordinator checks the vehicle type before fixing the date. Fog on the Yamuna Expressway can also slow long-distance trucks at night in winter.

A few papers make the day easier. Get the AOA move-out form and the no-dues slip at the old society and the move-in approval at the new one, keep a copy of your ID and the new address for the transport papers, and settle the rent deposit with the landlord before the truck leaves. If a car with a UP 16 number is going to another state, keep the registration certificate, insurance and pollution papers together, because they go on the inventory and are needed later for re-registration. If you need storage between two homes, it can be arranged through a partner warehouse, charged separately and shown on the estimate.

Common questions

What does a local house move inside Noida cost?

Within Noida, for example Sector 62 to Sector 137 or Sector 18 to Sector 76, a 1 BHK costs about Rs 5,000 to 9,000, a 2 BHK about Rs 8,000 to 15,000 and a 3 BHK about Rs 12,000 to 21,000, plus GST. Extra floors without a lift or a long carry from the gate are shown as their own line on the written estimate.

Is a move from Noida to Delhi or Greater Noida charged as local?

Yes. Delhi, Greater Noida, Greater Noida West and Faridabad are all on the local band with Noida, because they are treated as one metro area. A 2 BHK costs about Rs 8,000 to 15,000 plus GST on this band. A move to Ghaziabad gets a written figure after the survey. For Delhi, the loading and delivery hours follow the goods vehicle entry rules on the roads concerned.

How much does it cost to shift from Noida to Gurugram?

Moves between Noida and Gurugram are not priced on the local band. They are quoted after a survey, because the cost depends on both addresses and the route the truck takes across or around Delhi. Call the 24-hour line, share both addresses and a rough list of goods, and you get a written figure plus GST.

Do you have an office in Noida?

No. We have no office, branch or warehouse in Noida. Moves here are carried out by a verified partner team and coordinated from our head office at #705, Sector-7, Panchkula. The estimate and the GST invoice carry our name and GSTIN, and one coordinator handles your calls from start to finish.

My AOA allows moves only at fixed hours. Can you work around that?

Yes. Share the AOA rules with the coordinator before the date is fixed. The partner team arrives inside the permitted window, uses the service lift booked for you, pads the lift and protects the lobby floor. If the society allows moves only on weekdays, the date is planned around that first.

Can you shift a single room from a Noida urban village or PG?

Yes. A room with a bed, a table, a few cartons and a bike is priced as a 1 RK, about Rs 3,000 to 6,500 plus GST inside Noida and the local band area. Where the lane is narrow, goods are carried to the sector road or shuttled in a small vehicle, and that is written on the estimate.

Can I shift from Noida during the winter smog restrictions?

Usually yes. Moves inside Noida and to Greater Noida are usually less affected than moves into Delhi, but the coordinator checks the vehicle type against the GRAP stage in force. For a move into Delhi while the stricter stages of GRAP are in force, older diesel trucks may not be allowed in, so the coordinator arranges a vehicle that meets the rules or suggests a nearby date.

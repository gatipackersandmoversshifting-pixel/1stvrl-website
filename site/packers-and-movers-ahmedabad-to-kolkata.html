From Ahmedabad the truck goes south on NH48 to the Surat side and turns east on NH53, crossing north Maharashtra through Navapur, Dhule, Jalgaon, Akola and Amravati to Nagpur. It continues on NH53 into Chhattisgarh through Durg and Raipur, crosses into Odisha towards Sambalpur, and then runs north-east into Bengal, joining NH16 near Kharagpur for the last stretch into Kolkata. The distance is about 2,000 km, so this move is in the 1,500 km plus band of our chart. A shared truck usually delivers in 7 to 9 days.

Drivers usually make three night halts, often around Dhule or Jalgaon, near Nagpur, and around Raipur or Sambalpur. NH53 across Maharashtra and Chhattisgarh is a busy freight road with many trucks, and parts of the stretch through Odisha pass through forest and hill country that the driver prefers to cover in daylight. In the monsoon, heavy rain in central India can slow the trip, so a day of buffer is built in. Tolls are shown together as one line, and entry into Kolkata is timed to the city's goods vehicle rules.

Moving into Kolkata: what to plan for

Kolkata has two very different kinds of homes. Salt Lake, New Town and Rajarhat have planned blocks and towers with lifts and society rules much like Ahmedabad's newer suburbs. Older parts of north and south Kolkata, from Shyambazar to Behala, have old buildings with steep stairs, often without a lift, on lanes too narrow for a big truck. The city is humid all year, and the monsoon from June to September can leave streets waterlogged. During Durga Puja in September or October many roads close for pandals.

Common delivery areas are Salt Lake, Sector V, New Town, Rajarhat, Lake Town, Dum Dum, Baguiati, Ballygunge, Alipore, Kasba, Jadavpur, Garia, Behala, Tollygunge and Howrah.

Our tip for this route: Try not to plan the Kolkata unloading during the main Durga Puja days. Road closures and crowds slow access, and some housing societies do not allow moves during the festival.

Who shifts from Ahmedabad to Kolkata

Many people on this route are going home. Bengali families who came to Ahmedabad for jobs in banks, textiles, engineering or teaching often return to be near parents or to buy a flat in Kolkata. Others are moving for work: bank, FMCG and IT staff transferred to offices in Salt Lake Sector V and New Town, and central government officers on posting. Traders with links in both cities also shift households on this route.

Returning families often carry old wooden almirahs, books, a harmonium or other instruments and many years of household goods. These need careful packing, with corrugated sheet and padding at every corner. For Ahmedabad to Kolkata packers and movers, listing such items at the survey lets us size the truck correctly and put the delicate pieces on the inventory sheet by name, so they are the first things you check in Kolkata.

Packing for a 2,000 km trip across central India

The truck crosses several states and climates, from dry Gujarat heat to the humid air of the Bengal plains. Clothes, books and documents go in plastic-lined cartons, steel almirahs get corner padding, and leather sofas and polished wood are wrapped in film over corrugated sheet. A few silica gel sachets in cartons of papers and photo albums help in the first damp weeks, and cartons are best opened soon after unloading.

If the Kolkata address is in an older building, the partner team takes heavy furniture apart before carrying it up and sends extra hands for the stairs. Where a big truck cannot enter the lane, goods are moved into a smaller vehicle at the main road, and that transfer is shown on the estimate as its own line, not added on the day. The coordinator checks the lane before quoting so this is known in advance.

Common questions

How much do packers and movers charge from Ahmedabad to Kolkata?

For about 2,000 km, a 1 BHK costs Rs 20,000 - 35,000, a 2 BHK Rs 27,000 - 47,000 and a 3 BHK Rs 34,000 - 60,000, plus 18 per cent GST. The written estimate after the survey is the final price.

How long does Ahmedabad to Kolkata shifting take?

Usually 7 to 9 days on a shared truck from the loading day. Heavy rain in central India during the monsoon can slow the trip, and the coordinator tells you as soon as the expected date changes.

Our company is opening a Kolkata branch. What does the office move cost?

Sending up to 10 desks with chairs, files and computers comes to about Rs 55,000 to 90,000 plus GST on this route, and 10 to 30 desks about Rs 1,10,000 to 2,00,000 plus GST. For a bigger setup someone visits the office first and then gives a written figure.

Is delivery to Howrah, Barasat or Sodepur priced differently?

No. Any address in Kolkata or its suburbs on either bank of the Hooghly is charged from the same 1,500 km plus column. What can change is the last kilometre: a tight lane may need a smaller vehicle, and that is written on the estimate.

Can a sitar, harmonium or old teak almirah go safely to Kolkata?

Yes, if it is packed for it. Instruments go in their own cases or in padded cartons stood upright, and an old teak almirah has its doors tied and its mirror removed and boxed. Point these items out during the survey so foam and extra sheet come on packing day.

Can my car go to Kolkata on the same day as the house?

The car normally travels on a separate carrier, so it may arrive a day or two before or after the household truck. A hatchback costs about Rs 13,000 to 22,000 and an SUV or MUV about Rs 20,000 to 34,000 on this route, plus GST.

Surat to Hyderabad at a glance

970 kmRoad distance, approx.
4 to 6 daysHousehold transit time
NH48 to Pune, then NH65 via Solapur and ZaheerabadMain route
500 to 1,500 kmPrice band used below

From Surat the truck takes NH48 south into Maharashtra, passes the Thane and Bhiwandi side and climbs the Mumbai-Pune Expressway to Pune. It then turns east on NH65 through Indapur and Solapur, passes Umarga, crosses a short stretch of Karnataka around Humnabad, enters Telangana at Zaheerabad and reaches Hyderabad through Sangareddy and Patancheru. The road distance on this line is about 970 km, which puts the move on the 500 to 1,500 km band, and a shared truck usually delivers in 4 to 6 days.

Drivers usually halt for the night around Pune and sometimes again near Solapur. The expressway ghat near Lonavala is the slowest climb, after which NH65 east of Pune is mostly flat and quick through Indapur and Solapur. In summer the stretch across Solapur and the Telangana border is very hot by day, and in the monsoon short heavy showers can slow the run into Hyderabad. Tolls on all three roads are written together as one line.

Moving into Hyderabad: what to plan for

Most Surat moves end in west Hyderabad, where Gachibowli, Kondapur, Madhapur, Nanakramguda, Kokapet and Tellapur have large gated communities with strict move-in rules and booked service lifts. Pharma staff often head to the Patancheru, Bollaram and Genome Valley side instead. Hyderabad's summer is dry and very hot, and the rains from June to October can come as short, heavy bursts that flood low roads for a few hours.

Common delivery areas are Gachibowli, Kondapur, Madhapur, HITEC City, Financial District, Nanakramguda, Kokapet, Tellapur, Manikonda, Kukatpally, Miyapur, Bachupally, Begumpet, Secunderabad and Patancheru.

Our tip for this route: Gated communities in Hyderabad usually ask for a move-in form, a lease copy and sometimes a refundable deposit. Arrange these before loading in Surat, and the truck can be timed to reach on the day of your slot.

Surat to Hyderabad packers and movers: tech, pharma and PSU moves

Hyderabad's software campuses in HITEC City and the Financial District, and its pharma and biotech clusters, draw engineers, chemists and managers from Surat, many of whom worked in the chemical and textile processing units of Sachin, Pandesara and the Hazira belt. PSU and oil company staff on transfer form another steady group. Most of these homes are in Adajan, Pal, Vesu or Althan.

A typical load is a 2 BHK or 3 BHK with a car, and often a second vehicle such as a scooter. Because Hyderabad's west side is spread out, the delivery address matters for planning: a tower in Kokapet and a villa in Tellapur can need different vehicles for the last stretch. The surveyor notes this at the start so the partner team in Hyderabad sends the right truck.

What to pack, what to carry and what to leave in Surat

Keep your laptop, documents, jewellery and medicines with you. Everything else is packed by the partner team in Surat: clothes and books in cartons, electronics in foam, furniture in film and corrugated sheet, and glass and crockery in separate marked fragile cartons. Cartons are numbered and listed, so missing items are easy to spot on arrival.

Ask the surveyor to price the move with and without heavy items that are cheap to replace, such as an old cooler, spare plastic furniture or a large inverter battery. Hyderabad homes often have air conditioners, and the freight on these extras over 970 km can be more than they are worth.

Common questions

How much do packers and movers charge from Surat to Hyderabad?

For about 970 km, a 1 BHK costs Rs 15,000 - 27,000, a 2 BHK Rs 20,000 - 36,000 and a 3 BHK Rs 26,000 - 46,000, plus 18 per cent GST. The written estimate after the survey is the final price.

How long does shifting from Surat to Hyderabad take?

Generally 4 to 6 days from loading on a shared truck; a dedicated truck is quicker at a higher cost. The expected delivery date is written on the estimate.

Can a textile trading office with sample racks move to Hyderabad?

Yes. Racks and fabric rolls are wrapped and listed, desks and computers go on the office chart, where up to 10 workstations cost about Rs 40,000 to 70,000 plus GST, and trade stock that needs an e-way bill is listed against your firm's invoice before loading.

How is a bike packed for the Surat to Hyderabad trip?

The tank is run down to a little fuel, mirrors come off, and the bike is wrapped in foam and corrugated sheet before it is tied down on the carrier, with photos taken at pickup. An Activa costs about Rs 3,500 to 5,500 and an Apache RTR 200 about Rs 5,500 to 8,000 on this route, plus GST.

Why does the truck go through Pune to reach Hyderabad?

NH48 to Pune and then NH65 through Solapur is a wide, well-used truck road for this trip. Some drivers take inland roads via Nashik and Marathwada instead; the route is confirmed at dispatch and the band does not change.

Can you move lab or pharma office equipment to Hyderabad?

Office furniture, computers and files are moved on the office chart. Sensitive lab instruments and chemicals need the maker's or employer's own handling rules, so tell the surveyor about them and they are listed separately or left to the specialist.

Can I register my car in Telangana after the move?

Yes, if it will stay there beyond a year. You need an NOC from the Surat RTO and the usual papers. Keep the registration certificate, insurance and pollution certificate together.

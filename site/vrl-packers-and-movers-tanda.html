Shifting in Tanda: what we plan for

Tanda, officially Urmar Tanda, is a municipal council town made of two settlements about 1.5 km apart, Urmar and Tanda, with Ahiyapur and Darapur also inside the town limits. It sits at a meeting point of roads to Hoshiarpur, Jalandhar, Dasuya and Sri Hargobindpur, with onward links to Amritsar and Pathankot, and Tanda Urmar railway station lies on the Jalandhar to Pathankot line. The town is closely linked with Guru Hargobind, the sixth Sikh Guru: Gurdwara Pul Pukhta Sahib stands on the banks of the Kali Bein about 5 km away, and Gurdwara Tahli Sahib is also well known. Ram Mandir Ahiyapur, Shri Mahadev Mandir and the shrine of Sakhi Sarwar at Ahiyapur draw visitors too. Homes range from old houses in the Urmar and Tanda bazaars to newer kothis on the main roads. Villages such as Pul Pukhta, Munak Kalan, Kurala Khurd, Lodhi Chak and Ganduwal lie close by. Summers are hot and humid, the monsoon is heavy, and winter brings fog.

Home shifting in Tanda in the monsoon

July to September is wet in the Doaba, and low-lying roads can flood after heavy rain. For home shifting in Tanda in the monsoon, cartons are sealed on every seam, sofas and mattresses are wrapped in plastic, and loading is timed for a break in the rain.

Winter fog from December to January makes the early hours slow on the roads to Hoshiarpur and Jalandhar. Long-distance trucks set off once it clears, and the delivery window in your estimate allows for that. Summer moves, in contrast, start at first light so the heaviest carrying is done before the afternoon heat.

Locked houses and families abroad

Like much of the Doaba, Tanda has many families with members overseas. Often a house is being closed for a few years, or parents are moving to a child's home in another city. The survey separates goods that move, goods that go to a relative nearby, and goods that stay, and each lot is priced on its own line.

A relative's house in the same district within about 30 km is on the local band, while a long-distance lot is priced on its own band. Furniture left in the closed house can be wrapped and listed on a separate sheet, so whoever opens the house later knows what is inside. Keys, documents and jewellery are best carried by the family and are never packed into the truck.

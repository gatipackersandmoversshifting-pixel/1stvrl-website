Shifting in Mumbai: what we plan for

Shifting in Mumbai is mostly about access. A tower in Powai or Lower Parel will have a service lift but strict move-in hours, while an older building in Dadar, Girgaon or Bandra may have a tiny lift or none at all, and a chawl room is reached by a narrow common staircase. Most societies want an NOC or a written move-in permission from the managing committee before goods leave or enter, and the truck often cannot stand on the road outside for long, so the survey records the lift size, the floor, the stair width and where the vehicle can park.

We plan crews by the way the city is laid out: Western line suburbs from Bandra to Dahisar and on to Mira-Bhayandar and Vasai-Virar, Central line suburbs from Kurla to Mulund, Thane, Dombivli and Kalyan, and Harbour line areas such as Chembur, Vashi, Nerul, Kharghar and Panvel. Loading is timed around the society's lift slot and the hours when goods vehicles can enter, which usually means an early start or a late-evening load. From June to September every carton and piece of furniture gets an extra waterproof layer, and the truck is covered before it moves.

Common questions

How much do packers and movers charge in Mumbai?

Within Mumbai a 1 RK costs Rs 3,000 to 6,500, a 1 BHK Rs 5,000 to 9,000, a 2 BHK Rs 8,000 to 15,000 and a 3 BHK Rs 12,000 to 21,000, plus GST. If the building has no working lift, the stair charge is written as a separate line on the estimate.

Do you have an office in Mumbai?

No. 1st VRL Packers and Movers has no office or warehouse in Mumbai. Mumbai moves are carried out by a verified partner team and coordinated from our head office at #705, Sector 7, Panchkula. You deal with one coordinator and receive one written estimate and one GST bill from us.

Do I need a society NOC to shift in Mumbai?

Most Mumbai societies ask for an NOC or a move-in letter from the secretary, and some ask for a refundable deposit or a fixed lift slot. Get it from both the old and the new society before moving day, and we plan the loading time around what they allow.

Can you shift during the Mumbai monsoon?

Yes. Moves run right through June to September. Cartons and furniture get plastic and waterproof wrap, the truck carries a tarpaulin, and where possible we avoid loading during the heaviest spells of rain.

Do you cover Thane, Navi Mumbai and Vasai-Virar?

Yes. Local shifting covers the whole region, from Mira-Bhayandar and Vasai-Virar on the Western side to Thane, Dombivli and Kalyan on the Central side and Vashi, Kharghar and Panvel on the Harbour side. Moves from Mumbai to Delhi, Pune, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai and Kolkata each have their own rate page.

Shifting in Rewari: what we plan for

Rewari is a railway town at heart. Its junction joins several lines, and the old steam loco shed is now the Rewari Railway Heritage Museum, still home to working steam engines. Around the town, industry has grown fast: the Hero MotoCorp plant and the units at Dharuhera, and the HSIIDC industrial estate at Bawal along NH48, bring in engineers, supervisors and workers from many states. Families live in the old city near the bazaars, in Model Town and the HUDA sectors, and in new colonies on the Delhi, Jhajjar and Narnaul roads. Morning fog in December and January slows the highway, and hot dusty winds blow in May and June.

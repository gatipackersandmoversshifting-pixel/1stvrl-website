### What they do that helps them rank
- **Very wide page network.** About 60 state/city hubs (`/punjab-packers-and-movers`, `/chandigarh-packers-movers-punjab`), service-in-city pages (`/home-shifting-in-delhi`, `/office-shifting-in-delhi`, `/car-transportation-in-delhi`), 15 Delhi route pages, a "bill for claim" page.
- **Heavy internal linking.** Each city page links ~60 "<city> to <x>" pages; footer lists all states and cities.
- **Blog / resources** on buyer worries: moving scams, price list, bike packing, legal action against movers.
- **Trust blocks** high on the page: IBA code with a verify link, ISO certificate number, "how we work" 4-step process, stats, testimonials, press logos.
- **Lead form** asks moving time (within a week / 15 days / month) and moving type (within city / state / other state).
- City page has a price table by distance band, 11 FAQs and 10 reviews.

### Where we are already better
- Real local content (sectors, localities, highways, distances); their route pages name no towns, no distance, no transit time, no prices.
- Rate charts on every page + route calculator; their route pages have no prices at all.
- One phone number everywhere (they show 2–3 different numbers, even in the title).
- Clean heading order and JSON-LD (FAQPage, MovingCompany, Breadcrumb); theirs skip levels, no visible schema.
- No unbackable claims or superlatives; they use "No. 1", "best and cheapest", typos.

### What we must NOT copy
- 19+ years, registered trademark, press logos, "Govt authorized", own IBA code, branch offices, "best/No. 1". Their text.
